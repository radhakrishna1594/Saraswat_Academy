About Ganita Manjari Part 2

Ganita Manjari Part 2 continues the Class 9 Mathematics course after Part 1. It covers Chapters 9 to 14 and gives students opportunities to develop mathematical reasoning, data understanding, algorithmic thinking, geometry, algebra and mensuration.

Students should read the textbook carefully, work through examples and activities, and attempt exercises independently before using solutions for checking their work.

Part 2 contains six chapters: Chapters 9, 10, 11, 12, 13 and 14.

How to Study Ganita Manjari Part 2

  • Read the concept and examples from the textbook before attempting exercises.
  • Write complete steps for reasoning-based and numerical questions.
  • Use diagrams and graphs wherever the question requires representation.
  • Check mistakes after solving rather than immediately copying a solution.
  • Revise difficult questions regularly and practise mixed questions before examinations.
