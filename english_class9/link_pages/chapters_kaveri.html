Class 9 English — Kaveri

“Kaveri” is the new NCERT English language textbook for Class 9 students for the academic session 2026–27. Developed in accordance with the vision of the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020 and the National Curriculum Framework for School Education (NCF-SE) 2023, the book aims to develop students' language proficiency, critical thinking, creativity and ability to communicate effectively.

Kaveri presents a rich combination of stories, poems and other literary and informative texts. Its themes explore human relationships, Indian culture and heritage, social values, aspirations, environment, science and technology, ethics and everyday experiences. The book encourages students not only to understand a text but also to analyse ideas, express opinions, make connections and reflect on the world around them.

Key Features of Kaveri

  • Develops reading, writing, listening and speaking skills through meaningful activities.
  • Promotes critical thinking, reasoning, reflection and effective communication.
  • Includes a diverse selection of stories, poems, articles and other forms of writing.
  • Connects English language learning with Indian culture, heritage and contemporary life.
  • Develops vocabulary and language structures through meaningful contexts rather than isolated memorisation.
  • Includes speaking, listening and writing activities to strengthen practical communication skills.
  • Encourages students to go beyond the textbook through additional reading, reflection and exploration.
  • Uses QR-code-based digital resources to provide opportunities for extended learning.
  • Encourages values such as empathy, resilience, responsibility and respect for others.

Language Skills Developed Through Kaveri

Kaveri treats language learning as an integrated process. Students work with texts to develop reading comprehension, vocabulary, grammar in context, listening, speaking and writing. The activities gradually encourage learners to move from understanding information to analysing ideas, forming opinions and responding thoughtfully.

  • Reading: Understanding, analysing and interpreting different texts.
  • Vocabulary: Learning words and expressions through context.
  • Grammar: Understanding language structures through meaningful usage.
  • Listening: Developing the ability to listen carefully and respond appropriately.
  • Speaking: Expressing ideas, opinions and responses confidently.
  • Writing: Developing clear, organised and purposeful written expression.
  • Critical Thinking: Analysing information, evaluating ideas and making connections.

Why Kaveri is Important for Class 9 Students

Kaveri is designed to help students become confident and thoughtful users of English. Instead of treating English merely as a subject for examination, the textbook develops the ability to understand different perspectives, communicate ideas clearly and respond critically to literary and real-world situations.

For Class 9 students, the book provides an important foundation for higher-level reading, writing and communication. Regular practice with the chapters, poems, vocabulary and language activities can improve comprehension, expression and confidence in using English in academic and everyday situations.

Frequently Asked Questions — Class 9 English Kaveri

1. What is Kaveri in Class 9 English?

Kaveri is the new NCERT English textbook for Class 9, introduced for the academic session 2026–27. It is designed to develop students' reading, writing, listening, speaking and critical thinking skills.

2. Is Kaveri the new NCERT English book for Class 9?

Yes. Kaveri is the new NCERT English textbook prescribed for Class 9 from the 2026–27 academic session. It replaces the earlier Beehive and Moments textbook structure.

3. Who publishes the Kaveri textbook?

Kaveri is published by the National Council of Educational Research and Training (NCERT).

4. How many units are there in Kaveri Class 9 English?

Kaveri contains 8 units. Each unit brings together a prose text and a poem or companion literary text around related themes.

5. How many prose texts are there in Kaveri?

Kaveri contains 8 prose texts, distributed across its eight units.

6. How many poems are included in Kaveri?

The book contains 8 poems, with each unit including a prose selection and a related poetic or literary text.

7. What are the prose chapters in Kaveri Class 9?

The eight prose texts are: How I Taught My Grandmother to Read, The Pot Maker, Winds of Change, Vitamin-M, The World of Limitless Possibilities, Twin Melodies, Carrier of Words and Follow That Dream.

8. What poems are included in Kaveri?

The poetic texts include Bharat Our Land, Gifts of Grace: Honouring Our Vocations, Canvas of Soil, I Cannot Remember My Mother, Nine Gold Medals, A Friend Found in Music, Words and Believe in Yourself.

9. Is Kaveri different from Beehive and Moments?

Yes. Kaveri is a new integrated English textbook for Class 9. It replaces the earlier combination of Beehive and Moments and follows a different unit structure and learning approach.

10. What is the main objective of Kaveri?

The main objective is to develop effective language skills along with comprehension, critical thinking, creativity, communication and appreciation of literature.

11. What skills does Kaveri develop?

Kaveri develops reading, writing, listening and speaking skills. It also encourages vocabulary development, critical thinking, reflection, interpretation and effective communication.

12. Does Kaveri include grammar and writing activities?

Yes. Language learning is integrated with reading and other activities. Students also work on writing and grammar as part of their Class 9 English learning.

13. Does Kaveri focus only on literature?

No. Kaveri combines literature with language development, comprehension, vocabulary, speaking, listening, writing and reflective activities.

14. Does Kaveri contain activities after the chapters?

Yes. The units include activities that encourage students to understand the text, respond to ideas, reflect critically, develop vocabulary and practise different language skills.

15. What type of questions can students expect from Kaveri?

Students may encounter comprehension, vocabulary, interpretation, inference, critical-thinking, application-based and literature-related questions. The activities also encourage students to express and justify their ideas.

16. Is Kaveri useful for Class 9 English exam preparation?

Yes. Students should study the prescribed texts carefully and practise the questions, vocabulary, writing tasks, grammar and comprehension activities associated with the syllabus.

17. How should students study Kaveri effectively?

Students should read each text carefully, understand its central idea and important details, learn vocabulary in context, practise the questions and activities, and revise important themes, characters, literary ideas and language skills.

18. Does Kaveri help improve English communication skills?

Yes. The textbook provides opportunities for students to practise speaking, listening, reading and writing, helping them become more confident and effective users of English.

19. Does Kaveri promote critical thinking?

Yes. Students are encouraged to interpret texts, analyse ideas, make connections, reflect on different perspectives and support their responses with reasoning.

20. What themes are explored in Kaveri?

The book explores themes related to learning, family, culture, heritage, vocations, nature, human relationships, aspirations, possibilities, music, language, dreams and self-belief.

21. Is Kaveri based on the new education framework?

Kaveri has been introduced as part of the newer curriculum framework and reflects the competency-oriented and holistic learning approach associated with NEP 2020 and NCF-SE 2023.

22. Can students find chapter-wise solutions for Kaveri?

Yes. Students can use chapter-wise study resources containing explanations, question answers, vocabulary, important questions, poem explanations and revision material based on the Kaveri textbook.

23. Are the old Beehive and Moments books still relevant for Class 9?

For students following the new 2026–27 Class 9 curriculum, Kaveri is the relevant NCERT English textbook. Students should check their current school syllabus before using older Beehive or Moments-based study material.

24. What is the academic session for Kaveri?

Kaveri is the new Class 9 English textbook for the 2026–27 academic session.

25. Where can students get Class 9 Kaveri study material?

Students can use reliable educational resources for chapter-wise solutions, notes, vocabulary, question answers, important questions, grammar, writing practice and revision material based specifically on the Kaveri textbook.
