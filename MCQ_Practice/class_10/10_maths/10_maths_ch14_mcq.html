Chapter 14: Probability

50 Board-Level MCQs with Answers and Solutions


Q1. Probability of an impossible event is

(a) 0
(b) 1
(c) 1/2
(d) 2

Answer: (a) 0


Q2. Probability of a sure event is

(a) 0
(b) 1/2
(c) 1
(d) 2

Answer: (c) 1


Q3. Probability always lies between

(a) -1 and 1
(b) 0 and 1
(c) 1 and 2
(d) -2 and 2

Answer: (b) 0 and 1


Q4. A coin is tossed once. Probability of getting a Head is

(a) 0
(b) 1/4
(c) 1/2
(d) 1

Answer: (c) 1/2


Q5. A die is thrown once. Probability of getting 6 is

(a) 1/3
(b) 1/6
(c) 1/2
(d) 1

Answer: (b) 1/6


Q6. The probability of getting an even number on a die is

(a) 1/6
(b) 1/3
(c) 1/2
(d) 2/3

Answer: (c) 1/2


Q7. Probability of getting an odd number on a die is

(a) 1/2
(b) 1/3
(c) 1/6
(d) 2/3

Answer: (a) 1/2


Q8. Probability of getting a prime number on a die is

(a) 1/6
(b) 1/3
(c) 1/2
(d) 2/3

Answer: (c) 1/2

Solution: Prime numbers are 2, 3, 5. Probability = 3/6 = 1/2.


Q9. Experimental probability is based on

(a) Observation of experiments
(b) Guessing
(c) Formula only
(d) Theory only

Answer: (a)


Q10. Probability of getting a number less than 7 on a die is

(a) 0
(b) 1/2
(c) 5/6
(d) 1

Answer: (d) 1


Q11. The probability of getting a red card from a deck of 52 cards is

(a) 1/4
(b) 1/2
(c) 1/3
(d) 2/3

Answer: (b)


Q12. A card is drawn from a deck. Probability of getting a King is

(a) 1/13
(b) 1/4
(c) 4/13
(d) 1/52

Answer: (a)


Q13. A card is drawn. Probability of getting a Queen is

(a) 1/4
(b) 1/13
(c) 4/13
(d) 1/52

Answer: (b)


Q14. Probability of drawing an Ace from a deck of cards is

(a) 1/13
(b) 1/4
(c) 4/13
(d) 1/52

Answer: (a)


Q15. A coin is tossed twice. Total possible outcomes are

(a) 2
(b) 3
(c) 4
(d) 6

Answer: (c) 4


Q16. Probability of getting two Heads in two coin tosses is

(a) 1/4
(b) 1/2
(c) 3/4
(d) 1

Answer: (a)


Q17. Probability of getting at least one Head in two coin tosses is

(a) 1/4
(b) 1/2
(c) 3/4
(d) 1

Answer: (c)


Q18. If probability of an event is P(E), then probability of its complement is

(a) P(E)
(b) 1 + P(E)
(c) 1 − P(E)
(d) 1/P(E)

Answer: (c)


Q19. Probability of getting a black card from a deck is

(a) 1/4
(b) 1/2
(c) 1/13
(d) 1

Answer: (b)


Q20. If P(E) = 0.25, then P(not E) equals

(a) 0.25
(b) 0.50
(c) 0.75
(d) 1

Answer: (c)


Q21. (CBSE PYQ) A die is thrown. Probability of getting a multiple of 3 is

(d) 2/3

Q24. Probability of getting a vowel from the word "MATHS" is

Q25. A bag contains 5 red and 3 blue balls. Probability of drawing a red ball is

Q33. The sum of probabilities of all possible outcomes is

(a) 0

Q34. Probability of drawing a red king from a deck is

(a) 1/13

(c) 1/52

Q35. Probability of getting a perfect square on a die is

(a) 1/6
(b) 1/3
(c) 1/2
(d) 2/3

Solution: Perfect squares are 1 and 4.

(d) 1/2

Q39. Probability of drawing a non-face card is

(a) 10/13
(b) 3/13
(c) 1/2
(d) 1/4

Q40. A die is thrown. Probability of getting a factor of 6 is

(a) 1/3
(b) 2/3
(c) 1/2
(d) 5/6

Answer: (b)

Solution: Factors of 6 are 1, 2, 3, 6.


Q41. Probability of selecting a consonant from the word "INDIA" is

(a) 2/5
(b) 3/5
(c) 4/5
(d) 1/5

Answer: (a)


Q42. (CBSE PYQ) Probability of drawing a Queen of Hearts is

(a) 1/13
(b) 1/26
(c) 1/52
(d) 4/52

Answer: (c)


Q43. If probability of success is 0.7, probability of failure is

(a) 0.2
(b) 0.3
(c) 0.4
(d) 0.7

Answer: (b)


Q44. Probability of getting an even prime number on a die is

(a) 1/6
(b) 1/3
(c) 1/2
(d) 2/3

Answer: (a)

Solution: Only 2 is an even prime number.


Q45. Which of the following has probability 0?

(a) Getting 7 on a die
(b) Getting 6 on a die
(c) Getting Head on a coin
(d) Drawing a card

Answer: (a)


Q46. Which of the following has probability 1?

(a) Getting a number less than 7 on a die
(b) Getting Head on a coin
(c) Getting 7 on a die
(d) Drawing an Ace

Answer: (a)


Q47. Experimental probability is calculated as

(a) Total Outcomes/Favourable Outcomes
(b) Favourable Outcomes/Total Outcomes
(c) Number of Trials/Successes
(d) Successes × Trials

Answer: (b)


Q48. If an event cannot occur, it is called

(a) Certain Event
(b) Complementary Event
(c) Impossible Event
(d) Experimental Event

Answer: (c)


Q49. The probability of an event E is denoted by

(a) E(P)
(b) P(E)
(c) PE
(d) EP

Answer: (b)


Q50. Which statement is true?

(a) Probability can be negative.
(b) Probability can be greater than 1.
(c) Probability lies between 0 and 1 inclusive.
(d) Probability is always equal to 1.

Answer: (c) Probability lies between 0 and 1 inclusive.

Solution: For any event E, 0 ≤ P(E) ≤ 1.
