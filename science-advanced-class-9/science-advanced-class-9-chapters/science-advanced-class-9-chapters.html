Science at Advanced Level (Optional) – Grade 9

Welcome to the solutions section for Science at Advanced Level (Optional), Grade 9 for the academic session 2026–27. The book is published by the Academic Unit, Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE).

This is a separate optional Advanced Science course alongside the regular Class 9 Science curriculum. It develops deeper scientific reasoning through Physics, Chemistry and Biology topics, with practical, analytical and application-based questions.
