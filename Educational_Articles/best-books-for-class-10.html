Essential Books to Score 99+ in CBSE Class 10 Board Exams 2027: Complete Subject-Wise Guide


Scoring 95% or above in the CBSE Class 10 Board Examination is not about collecting a large number of books. It is about choosing the right study material, understanding the syllabus, practising regularly and revising what you have already learned. A student with three well-chosen resources and a disciplined study routine can often perform better than a student surrounded by ten different reference books.

For the 2027 Board Examination, students should give first priority to the NCERT textbooks and the official CBSE syllabus. Reference books should then be used for additional questions, application-based practice, revision and mock examinations.

This guide from Saraswat Academy presents a practical subject-wise selection of books and resources for Class 10 students. The recommendations are designed for students targeting 95%+ while also keeping cost, workload and revision time in mind.


Why Choosing the Right Books Matters in Class 10

Class 10 is an important academic stage. Your Board marks become part of your academic record and may influence subject choices, school admissions and the way you approach senior secondary studies. However, a high percentage does not come from buying an expensive book. It comes from using the correct material at the correct stage of preparation.

The most effective sequence is simple:

  1. Understand the CBSE syllabus.
  2. Study the prescribed NCERT textbook thoroughly.
  3. Practise textbook exercises and important examples.
  4. Use one reliable reference book for additional practice.
  5. Solve competency-based and previous-year questions.
  6. Attempt full-length sample papers under examination conditions.
  7. Revise mistakes repeatedly.

Remember that 99+ is a target, not a guarantee. Your result will depend on preparation, accuracy, presentation, revision and performance in the actual examination.

General Study Strategy for a 99+ Score

1. Divide Your Preparation into Three Stages

A useful approach is to divide the academic year into three phases:

  • Learning Phase: Complete concepts and textbook exercises.
  • Practice Phase: Solve additional questions and chapter tests.
  • Revision Phase: Revise notes, mistakes, sample papers and weak topics.

2. Prefer Active Learning

Reading a chapter repeatedly does not necessarily mean that you have learned it. Close the book and try to explain the concept in your own words. Solve questions without looking at the solution and write short summaries from memory.

3. Maintain a Mistake Notebook

Whenever you make an error in Mathematics, Science, Social Science or a language, record the question and the reason for the mistake. Before the examination, this notebook can become one of your most valuable revision resources.

4. Do Not Study Every Book from Cover to Cover

A reference book is a resource, not another textbook that must always be completed completely. Use it according to your needs. For example, a strong Mathematics student may need only selected difficult problems from an advanced reference book.

5. Keep One Main Reference Book Per Subject

Using one primary reference book consistently is usually better than switching between multiple publishers. Additional books should mainly be used for question practice or sample papers.

1. English – Codes 184 and 101

Students should first identify which English course they are studying. Code 184 is English Language and Literature, while 101 is English Communicative. Class X students continuing the Communicative course should use the books and syllabus applicable to that course.

Primary Books

  1. NCERT prescribed English textbooks – NCERT.
    For English Language and Literature, the prescribed literature textbooks form the core of preparation. Read every chapter carefully and understand characters, themes, poetic devices, important events and textual details.
  2. All in One English Language & Literature Class 10 – Parneet Kaur and Neha Vats, Arihant.
    A useful secondary resource for theory, chapter practice, grammar, writing, competency-oriented questions and sample papers.
  3. Oswaal Class 10 English resource – Oswaal Editorial Board.
    Particularly useful during the practice and revision stage for chapter-wise questions and examination-style practice.

Important Areas

  • Reading comprehension
  • Grammar and language usage
  • Formal writing formats
  • Prose and poetry
  • Character and theme-based questions
  • Extract-based questions
  • Competency-based literature questions

Why These Resources Help

English requires both understanding and presentation. The NCERT text gives you the content base, while a good reference book exposes you to different question formats. For 99+, students should pay special attention to answer structure, grammar accuracy, relevant textual evidence and word-limit discipline.

Estimated completion time: 6–8 weeks for first-round preparation.

2. Hindi – Codes 002 and 085

For accuracy, Hindi Course A is Code 002 and Hindi Course B is Code 085. Code 087 belongs to Social Science. Students should select resources according to their actual Hindi course.

Primary Books

  1. Prescribed NCERT Hindi textbooks – NCERT.
    Students should study the prescribed literature books thoroughly instead of depending entirely on guidebooks.
  2. All in One Hindi-A / Hindi-B Class 10 – Arihant author teams.
    The current 2026–27 editions provide structured explanations, practice exercises, competency-based questions, revision material and sample papers.
  3. Oswaal Hindi question-bank resources – Oswaal Editorial Board.
    Useful for additional practice after the textbook syllabus has been completed.

What to Practise

  • Unseen passages
  • Grammar
  • Writing formats
  • Literary interpretation
  • Extract-based questions
  • Long and short answers
  • Important textbook themes and characters

Hindi students often lose marks because of weak answer presentation, unnecessary repetition or grammatical mistakes. Read model answers for structure, but write answers in your own clear language.

Estimated completion time: 6–8 weeks.

3. Mathematics – Code 041

Mathematics is one of the subjects where a carefully selected practice book can make a substantial difference. However, NCERT should remain the starting point. Students should solve the textbook examples and exercises before moving to advanced practice.

Primary Books

  1. NCERT Mathematics Class 10 – NCERT.
    This should be completed thoroughly, including examples, exercises and relevant activities.
  2. Mathematics Class 10 – R.D. Sharma.
    Particularly useful for students who want extensive numerical practice and additional exposure to different levels of questions.
  3. Secondary School Mathematics for Class 10 – R.S. Aggarwal and V. Aggarwal.
    A useful supplementary source for additional problem-solving practice.

Useful Practice Materials

  • Arihant All in One Mathematics
  • Oswaal Mathematics question-bank resources
  • Educart question banks and sample papers
  • CBSE official sample papers and marking schemes

Important Areas

  • Real Numbers
  • Polynomials
  • Pair of Linear Equations
  • Quadratic Equations
  • Arithmetic Progressions
  • Triangles
  • Coordinate Geometry
  • Introduction to Trigonometry and Applications
  • Circles
  • Areas Related to Circles
  • Surface Areas and Volumes
  • Statistics
  • Probability

Calculation Strategy

Do not rely excessively on shortcuts before understanding the standard method. First learn the correct procedure, then improve speed. Maintain a formula sheet and practise calculations without a calculator.

Estimated completion time: 10–14 weeks for strong first-round preparation.

4. Science – Code 086

Science requires a combination of conceptual understanding, numerical practice, scientific terminology, diagrams and application-based questions. The best strategy is to study Physics, Chemistry and Biology systematically rather than treating Science as one large subject.

Primary Books

  1. Science Class 10 – NCERT.
    This is the essential starting point. Read explanations, activities, diagrams, tables and in-text questions carefully.
  2. All in One Science Class 10 – Alpesh Kumar, Tamanna Sharma and Subhanjana Mukherjee, Arihant.
    The current 2026–27 edition combines theory, practice, competency-based questions, mind maps and sample-paper preparation.
  3. Lakhmir Singh & Manjit Kaur Science series.
    Useful for students who want detailed explanations and additional conceptual and numerical practice, particularly in Physics and Chemistry.

Physics Preparation

Focus on formulas, ray diagrams, numerical problems, circuits, laws and applications. Practise writing complete steps in numerical questions.

Chemistry Preparation

Learn chemical equations, reaction types, properties, observations and important concepts. Do not simply memorise equations without understanding what they represent.

Biology Preparation

Use NCERT diagrams extensively. Learn processes in sequence and practise explaining biological mechanisms in concise, scientifically correct language.

Lab Manual and Diagrams

Practical work should not be ignored. A good laboratory manual can help students understand experiments, observations and procedures. Practise important labelled diagrams separately because clear diagrams can improve answer presentation.

Estimated completion time: 10–12 weeks.

5. Social Science – Code 087

Social Science is often considered a memory-heavy subject, but high-scoring students usually study it through concepts, timelines, maps, comparisons, examples and structured answers.

Primary Books

  1. Prescribed NCERT Social Science textbooks – NCERT.
    History, Geography, Political Science and Economics should be read directly from the prescribed textbooks.
  2. All in One Social Science Class 10 – Kriti Arora and Sushmita Dhar, Arihant.
    Useful for chapter explanations, competency questions, revision tools, mind maps and examination-oriented practice.
  3. Xam Idea Social Science Class 10 – Xam Idea Editorial Board, VK Global Publications.
    Helpful for additional questions, self-assessment and competency-focused practice.

Subject-Wise Strategy

  • History: Prepare events in chronological order and understand causes, developments and consequences.
  • Geography: Combine textual learning with maps, locations, resources and data.
  • Political Science: Learn concepts through examples and comparisons.
  • Economics: Understand definitions, relationships, examples and real-life applications.

Map Practice

Keep a separate map-practice notebook. Spend a few minutes every week locating important places instead of trying to memorise the entire map shortly before the examination.

Estimated completion time: 8–10 weeks.

NCERT: The Book You Should Never Skip

For CBSE Class 10 preparation, NCERT should not be treated as an optional resource. It should be the foundation around which the rest of your preparation is built.

NCERT textbooks provide the prescribed academic content and are especially important for Science and Social Science. In Mathematics, the examples and exercises provide the basic framework on which additional problem-solving can be developed.

A practical rule is: NCERT first → reference book second → question bank third → sample papers last.

NCERT provides official digital textbook access for Classes I–XII, allowing students to access the prescribed material even when a physical copy is unavailable.

Sample Papers and Mock Tests

Sample papers should not be used only during the final week. Begin chapter and sectional tests after completing major portions of the syllabus and move towards full-length papers later.

The most reliable starting point is the CBSE official Sample Question Paper and Marking Scheme. These resources help students understand the expected structure and style of the examination.

Commercial sample-paper books from Oswaal, Educart, Arihant and other established publishers can then provide additional practice.

How to Analyse a Mock Test

  1. Check every incorrect answer.
  2. Identify whether the error was conceptual or careless.
  3. Record questions that consumed too much time.
  4. Rewrite important incorrect answers.
  5. Repeat the weak topic after one or two days.

Digital Resources and Online Learning

Students can supplement their physical books with reliable digital resources. Useful options include official CBSE academic resources, NCERT digital textbooks, DIKSHA learning material and educational YouTube channels.

Useful Online Sources

  • CBSE Academic resources and sample papers
  • NCERT digital textbooks
  • DIKSHA learning resources
  • Official educational webinars
  • Reputed Class 9–10 educational YouTube channels

Popular educational channels can be useful for difficult concepts, but students should avoid watching video after video without solving questions. A good rule is: learn → close the video → solve independently.

What High-Scoring Students Do Differently

There is no secret book that guarantees 99+. Students who consistently achieve high marks generally follow a few practical habits.

  • They finish the prescribed textbook before chasing advanced material.
  • They revise chapters multiple times instead of reading them only once.
  • They practise writing answers rather than only reading solutions.
  • They analyse mistakes after tests.
  • They maintain subject-wise short notes.
  • They practise time management before the final examination.
  • They avoid changing books repeatedly.
  • They focus on accuracy instead of attempting questions carelessly.

Common Mistakes in Book Selection

Mistake 1: Buying Too Many Books

More books do not automatically mean more preparation. Select one main reference resource and use additional books only when there is a specific purpose.

Mistake 2: Ignoring NCERT

Students sometimes jump directly to guidebooks. This creates unnecessary confusion because the student is learning from a secondary explanation before understanding the prescribed material.

Mistake 3: Using an Old Edition Without Checking the Syllabus

Always compare a reference book with the syllabus applicable to your examination year. A book designed for an earlier session may contain outdated chapter coverage or question patterns.

Mistake 4: Reading Solutions Instead of Solving

If you immediately read the answer whenever a question looks difficult, your brain does not get enough problem-solving practice. Give yourself sufficient time before checking the solution.

How to Avoid Information Overload

Final 99+ Checklist

  • Complete every prescribed NCERT textbook.
  • Finish textbook exercises before advanced reference questions.
  • Keep one reliable reference book for each major subject.
  • Complete chapter-wise question-bank practice.
  • Solve previous-year questions.
  • Practise CBSE sample papers.
  • Maintain a mistake notebook.
  • Revise formulas regularly in Mathematics and Science.
  • Practise diagrams and maps.
  • Improve answer presentation.
  • Attempt timed mock examinations.
  • Revise rather than continuously adding new study material.

Final Word: The Best Book Is the One You Actually Complete

A 99+ score in Class 10 does not depend on finding a magical guidebook. It depends on understanding the syllabus, studying the prescribed material thoroughly, practising different question types and revising intelligently.

Start with NCERT. Add one dependable reference book. Use question banks to identify gaps. Move to previous-year questions and sample papers when your syllabus is under control. Finally, spend more time revising your mistakes than searching for another book.

Students preparing for the CBSE Class 10 Board Examination 2027 still have enough time to build a strong preparation system. Do not measure your progress only by the number of chapters completed. Measure it by how confidently you can solve, explain and revise those chapters.

Choose fewer resources, use them deeply, practise consistently and revise repeatedly. That is a much more realistic path to 99+ than collecting books.

Important Note About Book Editions

This article is intended for students appearing for the CBSE Class 10 Board Examination 2027. Always purchase or access an edition that matches the syllabus applicable to your examination year.

Older 2024–25 or 2025–26 books may still contain useful practice material, but they should not automatically be treated as the latest edition for the 2027 examination. Before buying a commercial reference book, check its stated syllabus year and compare its chapter coverage with the current CBSE curriculum.

Book prices mentioned in this guide are approximate and can change according to publisher, edition, seller, discounts and availability.
