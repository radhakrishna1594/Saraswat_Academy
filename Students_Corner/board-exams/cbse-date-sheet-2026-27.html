Examination timings and important instructions

  • Start time: Scheduled morning papers begin at 10:30 AM. End time and duration vary by subject.
  • Reporting: Reach the centre well before the reporting time printed on your admit card. The paper start time is not the reporting time.
  • Admit card: Carry your valid admit card and follow the instructions from your school and examination centre.
  • Stationery: Keep permitted stationery ready. Check subject-specific instructions for calculators, geometry instruments and other permitted materials.
  • Electronic devices: Mobile phones, smart watches and unauthorised electronic or communication devices are not permitted inside the examination centre.
  • Subject code: Match both subject name and code. Similar subject names may refer to different courses or levels.
  • Practical/internal assessment: For regular schools, practical examinations, project assessment and internal assessment are scheduled during the January–February 2027 period. Confirm your exact date with your school. Winter-bound schools follow a separate schedule.

How to prepare using the date sheet

  1. Write down only the papers you have selected, using the exact subject codes from your LOC or admit card.
  2. Prepare a revision calendar that gives enough time to subjects with the earliest examination dates.
  3. Use gaps between papers for focused revision, timed sample papers and correction of weak topics.
  4. Complete practical files, projects and internal-assessment requirements according to your school's schedule.
  5. Practise writing answers within the time limit and reserve the final days for revision.

Frequently asked questions

When was the CBSE Date Sheet 2027 released?

The Class 10 and Class 12 main board examination date sheet for 2026–27 was released on 6 November 2026.

When will Class 10 examinations begin?

The Class 10 schedule begins on 16 February 2027 with Painting. Mathematics Standard and Mathematics Basic are scheduled for 17 February 2027.

When will Class 12 examinations begin?

Class 12 examinations begin on 16 February 2027 with Physics.

Are all papers of the same duration?

No. Examination duration depends on the subject. Check the official PDF and admit card for the exact duration and end time.

Where can I download the complete official date sheet?

Use the official CBSE PDF link in the Important Dates section above. It contains the full subject-wise timetable and is the final reference.
