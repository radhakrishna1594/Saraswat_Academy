Frequently Asked Questions about Poornima University Jaipur

Where is Poornima University located?

Poornima University is located at Ramchandrapura, Vidhani Vatika, Sitapura Extension, Jaipur, Rajasthan 303905.

When was Poornima University established?

Poornima University was established in 2012 by an Act of the Rajasthan State Legislature.

Is Poornima University recognised by UGC?

Yes. The university's current Academic Manual states that it is approved by UGC under Sections 2(f) and 12(B) and empowered to confer degrees under Section 22(1).

What courses does Poornima University offer?

It offers programmes in computer science, engineering, architecture, design, arts, management, commerce, sciences, public health, hotel management and other fields at UG, PG and doctoral levels.

Does Poornima University provide hostel facilities?

Yes. Separate residential facilities for boys and girls are provided, with multiple room categories and supporting amenities.

What is the annual fee for BCA?

The current BCA General programme page lists an annual programme fee of ₹1,20,000, plus the applicable one-time registration and other final-year charges.

What scholarships are available?

Scholarship routes include merit-based, JEE/NATA/NCHMCT JEE score-based, sports and other programme-specific scholarships. Current conditions should be checked for the selected course.
