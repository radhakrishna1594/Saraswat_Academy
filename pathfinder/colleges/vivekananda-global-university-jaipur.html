Vivekananda Global University Jaipur (VGU)

Vivekananda Global University, commonly known as VGU Jaipur, is a private multidisciplinary university established in 2012 at Jagatpura, Jaipur. This guide brings the important information a student or parent usually wants to know before exploring admission—courses, eligibility, fees, scholarships, campus life, hostel, placements and the admission process.

VGU Jaipur Admission 2026–27

Admission depends on the programme. VGU's 2026–27 admission information lists VGUCET as a university entrance route and also lists national or programme-specific examinations such as JEE, NATA, CLAT, JET, CAT, GATE and MAT for relevant courses.

  1. Choose the programme and check the latest eligibility conditions.
  2. Check whether the programme requires VGUCET or another entrance examination.
  3. Prepare academic documents and entrance-score documents where applicable.
  4. Complete the application through the official VGU admission channel.
  5. Check the offer/admission communication and fee schedule before making payment.
  6. Keep copies of the application, payment receipt and submitted documents.
Important: Admission dates and examination requirements can change. For 2026–27, VGU's official admission page should be treated as the final source for deadlines and programme-specific requirements.

Scholarships at VGU

VGU publishes scholarships based on different criteria, including entrance performance and special categories. Placements and Career Support

Placement information is an important part of university research, but students should read placement figures carefully and check the year, participating students, programme and definition used by the institution. Questions students often ask about VGU Jaipur

Is Vivekananda Global University in Jaipur?

Yes. VGU's campus is in the Jagatpura area of Jaipur, Rajasthan.

When was VGU established?

VGU was established in 2012 under an Act of the Rajasthan State Legislature.

Is VGU NAAC accredited?

Yes. VGU's NAAC certificate records an A+ grade with a CGPA of 3.29 on the four-point scale, valid through August 15, 2027.

What courses are available at VGU Jaipur?

VGU offers programmes across engineering, computer applications, management, law, sciences, agriculture, design, architecture, pharmacy, health sciences, humanities, media, hospitality and other areas, along with doctoral programmes.

What entrance exams are accepted?

The requirement depends on the programme. VGU's current admission information lists VGUCET and, for relevant programmes, examinations such as JEE, NATA, CLAT, JET, CAT, GATE and MAT.

Where should I verify the latest fees and admission dates?

Use the official VGU admission and fee pages because these details can change between academic sessions.
