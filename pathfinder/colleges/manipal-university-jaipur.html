Manipal University Jaipur (MUJ)

Manipal University Jaipur is a multidisciplinary state-private university at Dehmi Kalan, off the Jaipur–Ajmer Expressway. MUJ was launched in 2011 and offers undergraduate, postgraduate and doctoral programmes across engineering, computer applications, management, commerce, law, architecture and design, sciences, humanities, journalism, hospitality, pharmaceutical sciences and allied health sciences.

Admission to MUJ 2026–27

Admission criteria depend on the programme. MUJ's current eligibility information lists merit-based routes for several programmes and entrance-based routes for selected programmes such as B.Tech. The university currently references MET 2026 for B.Tech admissions, while eligible applicants may also have merit-based pathways for specified B.Tech categories.

Programme / areaCurrent admission information
B.TechMUJ lists MET 2026 rank-based admission for B.Tech programmes. Merit-based admission is also listed for candidates meeting specified academic/SAT/JEE criteria.
BBA / B.A. / selected UG programmesSeveral programmes use marks in the qualifying examination; some programmes may include a personal interview.

Central Library & Academic Facilities

The MUJ Central Library is described by the university as a 2,319 sq. m. air-conditioned facility with seating for 700. Its published collection includes tens of thousands of books, reference materials, journals and digital resources, with RFID-based library services and access to multiple e-resource platforms.

📖 Central Library

MUJ's current library information lists 57,800 books and 14,500 titles on one published overview, along with digital resources and research-support services.

🔬 Laboratories
