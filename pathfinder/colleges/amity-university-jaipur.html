Amity University Jaipur

If you are looking at private universities in Jaipur, Amity University Rajasthan is one of the campuses that usually comes up early in the conversation. The university is at Kant Kalwar on the Delhi–Jaipur Highway and has a large residential campus. It offers programmes ranging from engineering and computer applications to management, law, design, biotechnology, pharmacy, psychology, hospitality and the humanities.

What Can You Study at Amity University Jaipur?

This is one of the more varied parts of the university. A student looking at Amity Jaipur is not limited to engineering or management. The current programme catalogue covers a wide spread of subjects.

💻 Computer & IT

BCA, B.Sc. IT, B.Tech IT, B.Tech CSE and CSE specialisations such as AI & ML, Cyber Security, Data Science and Internet of Things, along with MCA and M.Sc. options.

⚙️ Engineering

Civil, Chemical, Mechanical & Automation, Electrical & Electronics, Electronics & Communication, Food Technology, Biotechnology and related engineering programmes.

📊 Management & Commerce

BBA, BBA International, B.Com, MBA and specialised management programmes in areas such as finance, marketing, international business, HR, analytics and digital marketing.

⚖️ Law

BA LL.B. (Hons.), BBA LL.B. (Hons.), B.Com LL.B. (Hons.) and LL.M. programmes are listed in the current programme information.

🧬 Life Sciences & Health

Biotechnology, Microbiology, Pharmacy, Physiotherapy, Food Technology, Clinical Psychology and related science programmes are available.

🎨 Design, Media & Arts

Fashion Design, Fine Arts, Interior Design, Animation & Visual Graphics, Journalism & Mass Communication, English, Liberal Arts and related courses.

🏨 Hospitality & Tourism

Bachelor of Hotel Management, BBA Hospitality Services & Event Management and tourism-related postgraduate programmes are part of the catalogue.

🏛️ Architecture

Bachelor of Architecture is offered through the Amity School of Architecture & Planning, subject to the programme's current eligibility and approval requirements.

🔬 Research

Ph.D. programmes are offered in selected areas including sciences, engineering, management, law, architecture, humanities and other disciplines.

Admission to Amity University Jaipur 2026–27

The admission route depends on the programme. Some courses are largely based on qualifying marks, while others may ask for an entrance score, AMCAT, JEE or another prescribed selection route. For engineering, Amity's current programme information mentions national/state engineering entrance examinations or AMCAT for several B.Tech programmes.

  1. Choose the course first. Do not start with the fee alone.
  2. Read the programme-specific eligibility carefully.
  3. Check whether the course needs an entrance score, interview or another selection step.
  4. Submit the online application through the university's admission system.
  5. Keep your Class 10/12 or graduation documents and relevant entrance scorecards ready.
  6. Before paying the final amount, check the current fee structure, scholarship terms, hostel charges and refund rules.

Hostel & Campus Life

Amity Jaipur is a residential campus. The university describes separate accommodation for boys and girls, a large on-campus hostel, dining facilities, medical support, laundry, daily-needs stores and recreational spaces. The campus also has a large sports complex and a water-harvesting lake.

🏠 Residential campus

The university currently describes hostel capacity of around 3,100–3,200 students for boys and girls. Room categories and charges should be checked for the particular academic session.

🍽️ Food & daily needs

The campus includes food outlets, cafeteria facilities, laundry, ATM and departmental-store services, making day-to-day living easier for residential students.

🏃 Sports

The university highlights facilities including horse riding, golf putting, shooting, archery, tennis, football and billiards, along with other sports areas.

Library, Labs & Learning Environment

Amity Jaipur has a strong emphasis on practical work and research. Its current institutional information lists more than 120 hi-tech laboratories and extensive library resources. The university also describes research centres working in areas such as water studies, climate research, microbial disease diagnosis, IoT and quantum science.

📚 Library

The university's current pages list tens of thousands of books and a large collection of online journals and publications. Digital resources are available alongside the physical library.

🧪 Hi-tech labs

Laboratories support engineering, biotechnology, pharmacy, media, hospitality, business, science and other programmes.

🔬 Research centres

Research activity includes specialised centres and interdisciplinary work in areas such as water, climate, microbial technology, IoT and quantum science.

Placements & Career Support

Amity Jaipur has a dedicated placement and industry-interaction system. The university's current admissions material reports 466 recruiters across Amity Group placements in 2024 and a highest CTC figure of ₹52.8 lakh associated with Microsoft. These are university-published group-level figures and should not be treated as a guaranteed outcome for every Jaipur student or every course.

💼 Training & placement

Students receive career preparation, recruitment support and opportunities for industry interaction through the university's placement ecosystem.

🤝 Industry exposure

Projects, industry visits, workshops, mentorship and industry advisory interaction form part of the learning environment.

📊 What to ask before admission

For your specific course, ask about eligible students, placed students, median/average salary, internships and recruiters for the latest graduating batch.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Amity University Jaipur located?
