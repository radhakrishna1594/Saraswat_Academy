NIMS University Jaipur

NIMS University Rajasthan is a large multidisciplinary university on the Jaipur–Delhi Highway. It is quite different from a university that focuses mainly on engineering or management: NIMS brings medical sciences, dental, nursing, pharmacy, allied health, engineering, computing, management, law, hospitality, sciences and humanities together on one large campus.

That matters when choosing a course. If you are looking at NIMS, the first question should be which programme you want to study. Fees, entrance requirements, hostel charges and even the admission route can be very different from one programme to another.

How Admission Works

There is no single admission formula for NIMS because the university has such a large range of programmes. A student applying for B.Sc. General Science, B.Tech, B.Pharm and MBBS should not assume that the same eligibility or entrance process applies to all of them.

1️⃣ Choose the course

Start with the exact programme and read its eligibility, duration, fee and admission requirements.

2️⃣ Check entrance rules

Some professional courses have entrance requirements imposed by the relevant regulatory framework; others may use merit, university selection or another route.

3️⃣ Apply

NIMS accepts applications through its online admission system and its admission office.

4️⃣ Submit documents

Keep academic certificates, identity documents, photographs and category/entrance documents ready where applicable.

5️⃣ Check the offer

Before paying, confirm the exact course fee, hostel choice, scholarship, refund rules and any additional charges.

6️⃣ Complete joining formalities

After selection, complete fee payment, document verification, medical or other programme-specific formalities.

2026–27: NIMS's current website is advertising admissions for 2026–27. It also shows that programme closing dates and class-start dates are published on the live admissions page, so students should check that page rather than relying on an old admission calendar.

Hostel & Campus Life

Hostel is a major part of the NIMS campus because the university has a large residential student population. The current campus-facilities page lists 29 hostels with capacity for 16,100 students, including 15 boys' hostels and 14 girls' hostels.

🏠 Hostels

Separate accommodation is available for boys and girls, with different room categories and fee packages.

📶 Everyday facilities

NIMS lists Wi-Fi, wardens, common rooms, laundry, mess/cafeteria facilities, reading areas and daily-needs services.

🛡️ Security

The university lists biometric access, CCTV, trained security personnel and round-the-clock campus security.

🏥 Health support

The campus facilities include an emergency ambulance and on-site pharmacy, alongside the university's health infrastructure.

🏋️ Fitness

NIMS lists a fitness and wellness centre, gymnasium, swimming pool and indoor sports facilities.

🛒 Daily needs

The campus includes banks/ATMs, shopping, a convenience store, cafes and transport services.

These facilities are described on NIMS University's current campus and hostel pages.

Library, Labs & Learning Facilities

📚 Central Library

Large physical collection, reading areas and study support are available to students across disciplines.

Students in health and medical programmes learn within a university environment that includes hospitals and clinical facilities.

Research, Innovation & Industry Exposure

NIMS currently highlights 100+ research and innovation centres, 1,000+ patents filed, 2,500+ active research projects and 3,500+ research publications as figures from the previous year. These are university-published institutional figures, not a guarantee of an individual student's research or placement outcome.

The university also describes industry-integrated learning with certifications, projects, internships and partnerships involving organisations such as Google, SAP and EY. Students should ask their department which of these opportunities are actually available to their programme and batch.

Placements & Career Support

NIMS has a dedicated placement ecosystem and publishes placement-related information. The university homepage currently highlights a ₹1.2 crore highest overseas package and 45,000+ placements. These are university-published figures across its broader institutional ecosystem, so they should not be read as the expected salary or placement count for every course.

💼 Placement support

Career services include recruitment support, training and industry interaction.

🌐 Industry exposure

Selected programmes include internships, projects and industry certifications.

📊 What to ask

Before choosing a course, ask for course-wise placement data, eligible students, placed students, median/average salary and relevant recruiters.

The figures above are current university-published claims and should be checked against the latest course-specific placement information.

Scholarships

NIMS offers merit-based and need-based scholarship options. The exact scholarship depends on the programme and the student's academic record.

  • Merit-based scholarships may be linked to Class 10/12 or graduation marks.
  • Some programme pages publish their own scholarship slabs.
  • Students should check whether the scholarship applies to first-year tuition only or can continue in later years.
  • Read the continuation conditions before treating a scholarship percentage as the final cost.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is NIMS University Jaipur located?

NIMS University Rajasthan is located at Dr. B.S. Tomar City on the Jaipur–Delhi Highway, Jaipur, Rajasthan 303121.

When was NIMS University established?

NIMS University Rajasthan was established in 2008.

What courses does NIMS University offer?

NIMS offers programmes across medical sciences, dental, nursing, allied health, pharmacy, engineering, computer science, management, law, hotel management, applied and life sciences, humanities, journalism, public health and other fields.

Does NIMS University have hostel facilities?

Yes. The university currently lists 29 hostels with a combined capacity of 16,100 students, with separate boys' and girls' accommodation.

How do I apply to NIMS University?

Applications can be made online through the university's admission system or through the admission office. The exact selection process depends on the programme.

Does NIMS University offer scholarships?

Yes. Merit-based and need-based scholarships are available, with conditions varying by programme. Some current programme pages publish specific marks-based scholarship slabs.

Is NIMS University suitable for medical and health courses?

NIMS has a substantial medical and health-sciences ecosystem including medical sciences, dental, nursing, pharmacy, physiotherapy, occupational therapy and allied health programmes. Students should check the exact regulatory approval and current entrance requirement for the course they want.

Is hostel compulsory?

Hostel is not automatically compulsory for every student. The university's programme information can differ by course and residential arrangement, so confirm the rule for the selected programme.

Thinking about NIMS University?

Don't compare universities only by the size of the campus or the highest package. First compare the exact course, eligibility, total fee, hostel cost, scholarship conditions, approval requirements and the career path after that particular degree.
