MNIT Jaipur

Malaviya National Institute of Technology Jaipur is one of the major engineering and technical institutes in Rajasthan. It began in 1963 as Malaviya Regional Engineering College and became an NIT in 2002. In 2007, it was declared an Institute of National Importance under an Act of Parliament. Today, it is a centrally funded institute under the Ministry of Education.

If you are a student preparing for JEE Main and thinking about MNIT, the important thing to understand is that admission is not through a normal college application. For B.Tech and B.Arch, the route goes through JEE Main followed by JoSAA/CSAB seat allocation. That makes your entrance rank, category, programme preference and counselling choices especially important.

Courses at MNIT Jaipur

MNIT is not only a B.Tech college. Its academic structure includes engineering, architecture and planning, science, management and doctoral research. The institute's current academic listing includes the following undergraduate programmes.

💻 Computer Science & Engineering

B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering. The institute also has a separate Artificial Intelligence & Data Engineering department.

⚡ Electrical & Electronics

B.Tech programmes in Electrical Engineering and Electronics & Communication Engineering.

⚙️ Core Engineering

Chemical, Civil, Mechanical and Metallurgical & Materials Engineering are part of the undergraduate engineering portfolio.

🏛️ Architecture

B.Arch is offered through the Architecture and Planning department.

🎓 M.Tech & M.Plan

Postgraduate programmes include specialisations in areas such as renewable energy, chemical engineering, disaster assessment and mitigation, urban planning and other technical fields.

🔬 M.Sc.

M.Sc. programmes are offered in Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics through the institute's postgraduate admission route.

📊 MBA

MNIT also offers an MBA through the Department of Management Studies. Admission is handled separately from the JoSAA route.

🧪 Research & Ph.D.

Ph.D. programmes cover engineering, science, management, architecture, humanities and interdisciplinary research areas.

🤖 New-age areas

Students will also find dedicated academic and research work around artificial intelligence, data engineering, cyber security, energy, materials and disaster mitigation.

How to Get Admission to MNIT Jaipur?

This is the part students should understand clearly. MNIT does not have a general direct-admission process for its regular B.Tech programmes.

1️⃣ B.Tech / B.Arch

Qualify JEE Main and participate in JoSAA counselling. Vacant seats, if any, are subsequently handled through CSAB.

2️⃣ M.Tech / M.Plan

Admission is generally through CCMT for the regular programmes, subject to the current programme eligibility and counselling rules.

3️⃣ M.Sc.

Admission is through CCMN based on the prescribed IIT JAM route for the applicable programmes.

4️⃣ MBA

MNIT publishes a separate MBA admission advertisement and institutional selection process.

5️⃣ Ph.D.

Ph.D. admissions are conducted through the institute's own selection process, with notifications issued during the year.

6️⃣ International students

International admission routes include DASA and other government-supported channels depending on the programme and applicant category.

For 2026–27: MNIT's current admissions page links the 2026 JoSAA and CSAB information for UG admissions and CCMT/CCMN information for relevant postgraduate programmes. Always use the current counselling brochure for the year you are applying.

Campus Life at MNIT Jaipur

One of the things that is easy to miss when looking only at rankings and entrance cut-offs is the size of the MNIT campus. The institute describes a large green campus with academic buildings, hostels, sports facilities, a dispensary, banks, post office, shopping facilities, canteens and guest houses.

🌳 Large residential campus

MNIT's official profile describes a campus of about 317 acres with academic, administrative and residential facilities.

🏏 Sports

The campus provides sports and games facilities along with spaces for co-curricular activities.

🍽️ Food & daily needs

Canteens, a Nescafé outlet, banks, post office and shopping facilities are available on or around the campus.

🏥 Medical support

MNIT has an on-campus dispensary supervised by a medical officer, according to its campus-life information.

💡 Innovation

The campus includes the MNIT Innovation and Incubation Centre and several research centres supporting student and faculty projects.

🎭 Student activities

Technical, cultural and student-welfare activities form a regular part of campus life, with events and student societies running throughout the year.

Central Library

The Central Library sits in the centre of the institute and provides air-conditioned reading areas, reading cubicles, book stacks, reference sections and digital resources. The library uses KOHA software for its database and RFID/smart-card based book issue system.

The current library portal also shows ongoing additions to print books, e-books, journals and research databases, so students have access to both physical and digital study material.

Placements at MNIT Jaipur

MNIT has a dedicated Training & Placement Cell. Its work includes internships, industrial training and campus recruitment for UG and PG students. The placement season generally runs from August through March, and the institute says that nearly 200 companies visit the campus for interviews across sectors.

💼 Companies

The placement office works with companies from core engineering, IT, manufacturing, consulting, finance, management and R&D sectors.

🧑‍💻 Internships

Industrial training is part of the B.Tech curriculum, and the placement cell also facilitates internship opportunities.

MNIT Jaipur — Questions Students Often Ask

Is MNIT Jaipur an NIT?

Yes. The institute received National Institute of Technology status in 2002 and was declared an Institute of National Importance in 2007.

How do I get B.Tech admission in MNIT Jaipur?

For regular B.Tech admission, qualify JEE Main and participate in JoSAA counselling. CSAB handles the applicable subsequent vacant-seat allocation.

Does MNIT Jaipur offer B.Arch?

Yes. B.Arch is one of the current undergraduate programmes listed by the institute.

What is the B.Tech tuition fee in 2026–27?

For the General/OBC category with family income above ₹5 lakh, the published tuition fee is ₹62,500 per semester. Fee remission applies to eligible income and category groups.

What is the hostel fee at MNIT Jaipur?

For 2026–27, MNIT's published notice shows ₹52,000 for a new hostel entrant and ₹37,000 for an already-enrolled resident for the listed semester charges. The new-entrant amount includes refundable caution money and the one-time hostel/mess admission fee.

Does MNIT Jaipur have hostel facilities?

Yes. MNIT provides on-campus boarding and lodging, with accommodation capacity listed separately for boys and girls.

Does MNIT Jaipur have CSE?

Yes. B.Tech Computer Science and Engineering is one of the institute's undergraduate programmes.

What postgraduate programmes are available?

MNIT offers M.Tech, M.Plan, M.Sc., MBA and Ph.D. programmes across several disciplines.
