What is JECRC University known for?

JECRC University has grown from the JECRC educational foundation into a multidisciplinary university in Jaipur. Its academic portfolio now reaches well beyond engineering and includes computer applications, business, law, sciences, humanities, design, mass communication, economics, allied health and hospitality. The university also highlights industry collaborations, research, entrepreneurship and student activities.

💻 Technology & Engineering

Students can explore B.Tech and technology-focused programmes in areas such as CSE, AI, data science, cyber security, cloud computing and other emerging specialisations.

📊 Business & Management

BBA, B.Com and MBA programmes cover conventional business disciplines as well as newer areas such as fintech, analytics, digital marketing and family business.

⚖️ Diverse disciplines

Law, sciences, design, humanities, media, economics, health sciences and hospitality are also part of the university's current programme portfolio.

Admission to JECRC University 2026–27

The admission route depends on the programme. JECRC University's current material lists UG, PG, lateral/migration, executive education and Ph.D. admissions, while individual programmes can have their own entrance, interview, portfolio or qualifying-examination requirements.

Scholarships at JECRC University

JECRC University's 2026 prospectus describes tuition-fee scholarships for eligible students. For example, the published UG scholarship structure includes 15% tuition-fee scholarship for 70%–84.99% aggregate marks in Class 12 and 25% for 85% and above, subject to the applicable policy.

Special provisions

Frequently Asked Questions about JECRC University Jaipur

Where is JECRC University located?

It is located in Ramchandrapura Industrial Area, Vidhani, Sitapura Extension, Jaipur, Rajasthan.

When was JECRC University established?

JECRC University was established in 2012 under the JECRC University, Jaipur Act, 2012.

Is JECRC University recognised by UGC?

The university states that it is approved by UGC under Sections 2(f) and 12(B), with authority to confer degrees under Section 22(1).

What courses are available?

Courses span engineering, computer applications, business, commerce, law, science, design, humanities, mass communication, economics, allied health, hospitality and doctoral studies.

What is the campus size?

JECRC University describes its campus as approximately 32 acres.

Does JECRC University offer scholarships?

Yes. The university publishes scholarship provisions, including merit-linked UG tuition scholarships in its 2026 prospectus.

Where can I check the latest fees and admission dates?

Use the official JECRC University website and current 2026–27 prospectus/fee documents because fees and dates can change.
