What can students study at JNU Jaipur?

JNU has developed into a broad multidisciplinary university rather than a single-stream institution. Its current programme directory covers technology, business, computer applications, education, languages, basic and life sciences, pharmacy, media, fashion, law, nursing, allied health, agriculture, hotel management and medical sciences.

💻 Engineering & Computing

B.Tech programmes include CSE and specialised areas such as AI, data science and cyber security, alongside biotechnology, biomedical, food, mechanical and electrical engineering.

📊 Business & Commerce

BBA, B.Com and MBA pathways include conventional and emerging areas such as digital marketing, fintech and digital banking.

⚖️ Professional & Health

Students can explore law, nursing, pharmacy, allied healthcare, agriculture, education, hotel management, media and other professional disciplines.

JNU Jaipur Admission 2026–27

JNU uses programme-specific admission routes. The university currently promotes the Joint Entrance Cum Scholarship Test (JEST) and also lists merit, entrance tests, personal interviews and national examination routes depending on the programme.

  1. Select the programme and read its eligibility requirements.
  2. Check whether JEST, JEE, CLAT or another examination applies.
  3. Complete the online application with correct academic details.
  4. Prepare marksheets, ID and examination documents.
  5. Complete the test/interview or merit process where required.
  6. Before paying, verify the final offer, fee structure and refund rules.
Important: Admission dates and programme requirements can change. Use JNU's current admissions page and the individual course page as the final source for 2026–27.

Scholarships & Financial Assistance

JNU provides scholarship and financial-assistance information through its admissions system. The university promotes JEST as an entrance-cum-scholarship test, while scholarship rules can also depend on academic performance and programme-specific conditions.

🏆 JEST

The Joint Entrance Cum Scholarship Test is promoted by JNU as an admission and scholarship route for eligible applicants.

🎓 Merit support

Students should check the current scholarship table for the programme, qualifying marks, continuation conditions and applicable tuition benefits.

📄 Verify conditions

Scholarships can have documentation, academic-performance and renewal conditions. Confirm the current policy before relying on a scholarship amount.

Frequently Asked Questions about JNU Jaipur

Where is Jaipur National University located?

JNU is located on the Jaipur-Agra Bypass in the Jagatpura area of Jaipur, Rajasthan.

When was Jaipur National University established?

Jaipur National University came into existence in 2007.

Is JNU Jaipur recognised by UGC?

Yes. JNU states that it received UGC approval under Section 2(f) in 2008, and the university is listed in UGC's private-university records.

What is JNU Jaipur's NAAC grade?

The current NAAC certificate records an A Grade with CGPA 3.30/4.00, valid until April 24, 2029.

What courses does JNU Jaipur offer?

JNU offers programmes across engineering, computer science, business, education, sciences, pharmacy, media, fashion, law, nursing, allied health, agriculture, hotel management and other disciplines.

What is JEST?

JEST is the Joint Entrance Cum Scholarship Test promoted by JNU as an admission and scholarship route for eligible applicants.

Where should students verify current fees?

Students should use the individual JNU programme page and current admissions information because fees and admission rules can change.
