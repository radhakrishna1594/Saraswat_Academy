Campus, Hostel & Student Life

SGVU's current facilities page describes a 130-acre green campus with six hostel blocks and 1,200+ hostel seats. The university lists separate boys' and girls' hostels, Wi-Fi, security, recreation facilities, gymnasium, yoga, mess, sports facilities, library, laboratories and medical support.

🏠 Hostel

The current university information lists four boys' hostel blocks with 800 beds and two girls' blocks with 400 beds. Rooms are described as furnished, with recreation areas and support facilities.

📚 House of Wisdom

The central library is described as fully air-conditioned with books, journals, e-resources, automated access and reading facilities.

🏏 Sports & recreation

Facilities listed by SGVU include cricket, football, basketball, badminton, volleyball, gymnasium, yoga, swimming and an open-air theatre.
