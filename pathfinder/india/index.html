India

Explore Indian school, college, university, course, entrance examination and admission information through Saraswat Academy PathFinder. This category is designed as a scalable directory: individual institution, course and admission pages can be added without making the main PathFinder page difficult to navigate.

🏫 Institutions

Dedicated pages for schools, colleges, universities and other education providers can be organised here as the directory grows.

📝 Admissions

Pages can cover eligibility, application process, required documents, important dates and official admission information for a specific academic year.

🎓 Courses

Course pages can explain subjects, duration, eligibility, progression and relevant entrance routes using verified information.

💰 Scholarships

Scholarship pages can explain eligibility, application windows and official sources, with the relevant year clearly displayed.

🌍 Destinations

Location-based pages can organise education information by country, state, city or study destination.

🔗 Official Sources

Institution-specific pages should link to the official institution or admission authority so students can verify current information before applying.
