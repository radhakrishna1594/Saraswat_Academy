How PathFinder is organised

The structure is designed for hundreds or thousands of future pages without making the main page endless.

1

Add a detailed institution or guidance article.

2

Place it inside the appropriate PathFinder category.

3

Link it from the relevant category/index page.

4

Keep this main hub focused on discovery and navigation.

For students and parents

Admission information can vary by institution, programme, location and academic year. PathFinder pages should therefore clearly show the relevant year, source or official admission link wherever applicable, so readers can distinguish general guidance from institution-specific information.
