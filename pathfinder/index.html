HOW TO USE PATHFINDER

Search less. Understand more.

Start with a category, open the relevant institution or guidance page, check the academic year and eligibility, and use the official admission link before making an application. PathFinder is designed to organise information — not replace an institution's official admission notice.

FOR STUDENTS & PARENTS

Information should be clear, current and easy to verify.

Admission rules, fees, dates and eligibility can change by institution, programme and academic year. PathFinder pages should identify the relevant year and provide an official source or admission link wherever applicable.
