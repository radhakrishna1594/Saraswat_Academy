Maharani Gayatri Devi Girls' School, Jaipur

Maharani Gayatri Devi Girls' School (MGD) is a girls' school on Sawai Ram Singh Road near Ajmeri Gate in central Jaipur. The school was founded in 1943 by Maharani Gayatri Devi and today offers day-school, day-boarding and boarding arrangements.

About MGD Girls' School

The school was established on 4 July 1943. MGD describes itself as the first public school in India established for girls. It is affiliated with CBSE and also offers Cambridge International education options. The campus has more than 2,400 day scholars and day boarders, while around 300 students reside as boarders, according to the school's profile.

The school is managed by the MGD Girls' School Society. Its motto is “Our Utmost for the Highest.”

Admission

For the 2027–28 session, the school states that online registration opened from 15 September 2026 for Nursery (PP3), LKG, UKG, Classes I–IX and XI. Registration is subject to seat availability and school admission norms.

  • Registration and processing fee: ₹5,000, non-refundable.
  • Registration is required before a candidate can be considered for admission.
  • The school states that an entrance test or interview date will be communicated through the registered email address.
  • Admissions are not taken for Classes X and XII.
  • Original birth certificate and supporting documents are required during verification.

Parents should check the school's current admission notice before submitting an application because dates, eligibility and seat availability can change.

Academics and School Life

MGD provides education from the early years through senior secondary classes. The school combines classroom learning with co-curricular activities, sports, leadership opportunities and programmes intended to develop confidence and responsibility.

Senior students can follow CBSE pathways, while the school also lists Cambridge International education among its affiliations and academic options. Parents should confirm the subjects and combinations available for the session in which their daughter seeks admission.

Boarding and Day Boarding

MGD offers boarding from Class I onwards. The school profile states that day boarding begins from Class VI. Boarding is organised through five boarding houses: Lillian Hurst, Ashiana, Deepshikha, Maharani Indira Devi Bhawan and Rani Vidya Devi Bhawan.

Boarding can be relevant for families looking for a residential school experience in Jaipur, while day scholars attend as regular students.
