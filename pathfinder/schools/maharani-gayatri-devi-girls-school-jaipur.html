Maharani Gayatri Devi Girls' School, Jaipur

Maharani Gayatri Devi Girls' School (MGD), commonly known as MGD, is a girls' day, day-boarding and boarding school on Sawai Ram Singh Road near Ajmeri Gate in central Jaipur. Founded in 1943 by Maharani Gayatri Devi, the school offers CBSE education and also lists Cambridge International education among its academic affiliations.

About MGD Girls' School

Maharani Gayatri Devi Girls' School was founded on 4 July 1943 by Her Highness Maharani Gayatri Devi. The school describes itself as the first public school in India for girls. It is located in central Jaipur on Sawai Ram Singh Road near Ajmeri Gate.

The school profile states that MGD has more than 2,400 day scholars and day boarders, while almost 300 students reside on campus as boarders. Day boarding begins from Class VI and boarding is available from Class I onwards.

MGD is managed by the MGD Girls' School Society. Its motto is “Our Utmost for The Highest.” The school combines academics with sports, arts, leadership, community service and other co-curricular programmes.

Admission to MGD Girls' School

For the 2027–28 session, the school has opened registration from 15 September 2026 for Nursery (PP3), LKG, UKG, Classes I–IX and XI for day scholars. Boarder registration for Classes I–IX and XI is also open from 15 September 2026. The school states that there is no admission in Classes X and XII.

Registration fee and process

  • Registration and processing fee: ₹5,000, non-refundable.
  • Day-scholar applicants must register online, download the completed form and submit it with supporting documents during the dates specified by the school.
  • For Nursery (PP3) and Class I, the 2027–28 timeline provides for a public draw at the school campus.
  • For Classes II–VIII and IX, admission is vacancy-based and the school states that interview/entrance-test dates are communicated by email.
  • For Class XI, the school lists Class X pre-board results and an interview as part of the admission criteria.
  • Boarder applicants follow a separate admission process after registration.

Documents

  • Birth certificate issued by the Municipal Corporation or competent authority.
  • Previous-class report card/mark sheet where applicable.
  • Original Transfer Certificate where applicable at the beginning of the session.
  • Medical certificate for boarder admission as required by the school.
  • Relevant category certificates, such as sibling, alumni, staff or government-service documentation, where applicable.
  • Parents are asked to declare relevant health, physical, physiological, psychological and learning concerns with supporting documents where applicable.
Important: Registration does not guarantee admission. The school states that the Principal reserves the right to admit or reject a student and that false declarations can lead to cancellation.

Academics and Curriculum

MGD provides education from the early years through senior secondary classes. The school publishes separate CBSE and Cambridge curriculum/syllabus resources and lists both CBSE and Cambridge International Education among its affiliations/options.

Learning beyond the classroom

The school publishes activities covering science, mathematics, languages, exhibitions, MUN, community service, internationalism, student-led initiatives, counselling, leadership and personality development. Its gallery also records exhibitions, library activities, Cambridge programmes, industrial visits and other student events.

Boarding and Day Boarding

MGD offers boarding from Class I onwards and day boarding from Class VI. The school profile lists five boarding houses: Lillian Hurst, Ashiana, Deepshikha, Maharani Indira Devi Bhawan and Rani Vidya Devi Bhawan.

For 2027–28, the school says boarder registration opened on 15 September 2026 for Classes I–IX and XI. The boarding admission procedure requires original transfer/birth documents, medical certification and other applicable documents at reporting.

Campus and Infrastructure

The official MGD campus page describes a 26-acre campus in the heart of Jaipur. It includes the Main School Building, Art Cottage, Natya Ved dance and music department, Maharani Shiv Kumari of Kota Wing, House of Science, Maharaja Jagat Singh Wing, dining hall, guest rooms, rose garden and lawns.

Sports and Physical Education

MGD lists hockey, cricket, kho-kho, basketball, volleyball, taekwondo, swimming, yoga, horse riding, tennis, skating, badminton, table tennis, athletics and cross country.

The school organises inter-house matches, athletic meets and inter-school competitions. NCC is offered as a subject option, and the school publishes sports activities and achievements through its website.

School History

The school traces its history to 1943. Its official history page records the foundation of the main school building in 1944, later development of the House of Science and Maharaja Jagat Singh Wing, and the reinstitution of the school library in 2013 as the Ms Lillian G Lutter Memorial Library and Reading Room.

The school has marked major anniversaries including its 75th year in 2018 and continues to maintain its historic identity alongside newer academic, sports and student programmes.
