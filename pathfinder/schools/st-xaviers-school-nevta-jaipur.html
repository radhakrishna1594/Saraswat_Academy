St. Xavier's School, Nevta

St. Xavier's School, Nevta is a co-educational English-medium school on Nevta-Mahapura Road, Jaipur. The school was founded in 2015 and is managed by the Society of Jesus. It is affiliated with CBSE and serves students through the senior-secondary level.

About St. Xavier's School, Nevta

St. Xavier's School, Nevta is a branch school of St. Xavier's Sr. Sec. School, C-Scheme, Jaipur. The school website describes it as a Jesuit institution managed by the Society of Jesus and an English-medium, co-educational school.

CBSE records list affiliation number 1730818, school code 11167, foundation year 2015 and the first opening date as 6 April 2015. The current CBSE record lists the affiliation period through 31 March 2027. The school disclosure names Fr. Sangeeth Raj, S.J. as principal.

Admission to St. Xavier's School Nevta

The school publishes separate admission notices for different classes. For the 2026–27 Class I admission cycle, registration was online and the school stated that selection for Class I would be through a draw of lots, with no written examination.

The 2026–27 Class I notice gave the registration period as 10 December 2025 to 30 January 2026. It also required the printed application and supporting documents to be submitted at the school office after online registration. These dates belong to the 2026–27 admission cycle; parents applying for a later session should check the latest notice issued by the school.

Documents mentioned in the Class I notice

  • Child's Aadhaar card
  • Municipal birth certificate
  • Recent passport-size photograph and family photograph
  • UKG/Prep mark sheet or bonafide certificate
  • Parent address proof
  • Additional documents for sibling or Christian-category applications where applicable
Class I seats in the 2026–27 notice: 240 seats were listed, including UKG-promoted, reserved, sibling and general-category seats. The general category listed 140 seats, with 70 for boys and 70 for girls.

Academics and Senior Secondary Classes

The school follows the CBSE curriculum and offers education up to Class XII. The 2026–27 Class XI admission notice lists PCM, Commerce and Humanities streams.

📚 Classroom Learning

The school describes academic learning alongside activities intended to develop communication, discipline, creativity and participation.

🔬 Labs & Technology

The school's mandatory disclosure lists 11 laboratories, internet facility and 723 classrooms/classroom units in its infrastructure information.

Facilities and Campus

The school describes a campus with smart classrooms and technology-supported learning, innovation and experiential learning spaces, laboratories, sports and co-curricular activities. Its public disclosure records a campus area of about 20,234 square metres and 11 laboratories.

⚽ Sports

School calendars and activity records include cricket, athletics, kho-kho, kabaddi, badminton and other inter-house sports activities.

🎭 Activities

The school's published activities include art competitions, debates, recitation, theatre, science quizzes and other co-curricular events.
