St. Xavier's School, Nevta, Jaipur

St. Xavier's School, Nevta is a co-educational English-medium school founded in 2015 and managed by the Society of Jesus. The school is located on Nevta-Mahapura Road, Jaipur, and is affiliated with CBSE.

About the School

St. Xavier's School, Nevta is a branch school of St. Xavier's Sr. Sec. School, C-Scheme, Jaipur. The school website describes it as a Jesuit institution managed by the Society of Jesus, an English-medium and co-educational school situated away from the busy central parts of Jaipur.

CBSE's school record lists affiliation number 1730818, foundation year 2015, first opening date 6 April 2015, and senior-secondary status. The CBSE record lists Fr. Sangeeth Raj, SJ as the head of institution. citeturn0search5

Admission Information

Admission procedures vary by class and academic session. The published Class I notice for 2026–27 required online registration, followed by submission of the printed application and supporting documents at the school office. Academics and Curriculum

The school follows the CBSE curriculum and provides education through the senior-secondary level. The school describes its academic approach around safe learning, values, good behaviour, competence and participation in curricular and co-curricular activities. citeturn0search1
