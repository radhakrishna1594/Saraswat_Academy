Banyan Tree School, Jaipur

Banyan Tree School Jaipur is a CBSE-affiliated co-educational school in Sector 7, Mansarovar. Established in 2011 under the aegis of Ram Krishan & Sons Charitable Trust, the school provides education from the early years through senior secondary classes.

About the School

Banyan Tree School Jaipur was established in 2011 under the aegis of Ram Krishan & Sons Charitable Trust. The school describes its educational approach as progressive and values-based, with emphasis on curiosity, creativity, collaboration, leadership and experiential learning.

The school's CBSE mandatory disclosure lists affiliation number 1730621, school code 10967, the Sector-7 Mansarovar address and Ms. Surabhi Saxena (Officiating) as principal in the current disclosure.

Academics and Curriculum

Banyan Tree School Jaipur follows the CBSE curriculum and describes its academic programme around knowledge, creativity, critical thinking and experiential learning. The school also provides opportunities in languages, STEM, arts, sports and student leadership.

Primary & Middle School

Students build subject foundations through classroom learning, practical activities, projects, reading and co-curricular participation.

Secondary School

Classes IX and X follow the CBSE secondary curriculum with academic subjects supported by laboratory and activity-based learning.

Senior Secondary

Classes XI and XII form the senior-secondary stage. Current subject combinations should be confirmed with the school for the relevant session.

Languages & Enrichment

The school publishes learning resources and activities involving English, Hindi and additional language options, along with music, art, dance, debate and other enrichment programmes.

Campus and Facilities

The school's mandatory disclosure records a campus area of 11,391 square metres, 75 classrooms and 7 laboratories, each listed at approximately 139.72 square metres. Internet facility is also listed as available.

Laboratories

The school lists Physics, Chemistry, Biology, IT, Psychology, Robotics and e-lab facilities for practical and technology-supported learning.

Library

The library is described as a learning hub with a wide range of books and resources designed to support reading, teaching and independent learning.

STEAM & Robotics

The Robotics/STEAM facility provides students with opportunities to explore technology, problem-solving and practical projects.

Performing Arts

Dedicated art and music spaces support creative expression through music, dance, theatre and related activities.

Infirmary & Safety

The campus includes a modern infirmary. The school also states that CCTV coverage, fire extinguishers and security measures are part of its campus safety arrangements.

Sports

Published sports facilities and activities include badminton, volleyball, football, skating, yoga, judo, taekwondo, table tennis, carom, chess, athletics and a playing field.

Sports and Co-curricular Activities

Sports and activities are integrated into the school's programme. The school highlights cricket, football, basketball, athletics, yoga and indoor games, supported by coaches and dedicated sports facilities. Student life also includes music, art, dance, debating, leadership activities and inter-house events.

Student Council and House System

The school has a student council and a four-house system. The published student-council information identifies houses including Abhilasha, Aakanksha, Prerna and Prarthana. Students participate in leadership roles and inter-house activities across academic, cultural and sporting programmes.
