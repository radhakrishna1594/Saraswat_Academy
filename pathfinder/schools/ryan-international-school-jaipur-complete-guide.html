Ryan International School, Jaipur

Jaipur has three Ryan International School campuses listed on the Ryan Group’s official CBSE school directory: Mansarovar (V.T. Road), Padmavati/Nirman Nagar, and Goner Road–Jagatpura (Vidhani). This guide keeps their affiliation details, locations and school levels separate so parents can compare the right campus.

Researched from the Ryan Group website and CBSE SARAS records. Please confirm current admission availability, fee schedules, transport routes and timings directly with the selected campus.

Important: These are three separate campuses, not interchangeable branches. Nirman Nagar’s official CBSE record lists Secondary Level, while Mansarovar and Jagatpura are listed as Senior Secondary Level. Fee details, stream availability and admissions can differ.

Academics and curriculum

All three campuses are listed by Ryan Group under its Jaipur CBSE schools. The official curriculum pages describe English as the medium of instruction and a learner-centred approach built around activity-based, collaborative and experiential learning.

  • Primary and middle years: The group describes an emphasis on building confidence, curiosity, communication, judgement and social-emotional development.
  • Senior secondary: The Mansarovar and Jagatpura curriculum pages list Humanities, Commerce, and Science (Medical and Non-medical). Confirm the exact subjects and combinations available in the current session.
  • Nirman Nagar: Its campus page describes Classes I–X and its CBSE status is Secondary Level. Ask the school about the onward pathway for Class XI.
  • Learning approach: The group describes activity-based, collaborative and experiential learning, alongside student development through academic and co-curricular opportunities.

Do not choose a campus based only on the Ryan name. Check the actual class level, subject combination, commute and total annual cost for your child’s grade.

Facilities, innovation and student life

Ryan Group’s official curriculum and student-life sections describe a mix of classroom learning and co-curricular opportunities. Facilities can differ by campus and grade, so treat the following as areas to check on a campus visit rather than a guarantee that every item is identical at all three locations.

  • Innovation and STEM: The group’s curriculum describes innovation labs and Atal Tinkering Lab activities in its network. Confirm campus-specific equipment, access and age groups.
  • Sports and creative arts: Ryan’s official student-life menu lists sports and creative arts; ask for the current timetable, coaches, facilities and competition opportunities at each branch.
  • Student leadership and activities: The group describes leadership and experiential-learning programmes. Ask which clubs, events, trips and student councils are active locally this year.
  • Safety and well-being: Review the school’s current child-protection, visitor-entry, transport, emergency and medical procedures in person and through the latest mandatory disclosure.

Admission process and documents

Admissions are handled by each campus and seat availability can vary by class. The Ryan Group admission pages provide online enquiry/application options, but the academic-year notice visible on a website may change. Confirm that the application window is open for the current session before applying.

Suggested steps for parents

  1. Choose the exact branch and grade; confirm whether that campus offers the required class.
  2. Contact the campus through its official website and ask about vacancies, age criteria, assessment/interview requirements and the admission calendar.
  3. Request the current prospectus, fee schedule, transport routes and refund policy in writing.
  4. Complete the official application and submit documents only through the school’s authorised process.
  5. Keep copies of the application, fee receipts and all written admission confirmations.

Documents to keep ready

  • Child’s birth certificate and recent passport-size photographs.
  • Previous school report card / latest marksheet for transfer admissions.
  • Transfer Certificate or school leaving certificate, where applicable.
  • Child’s and parent/guardian’s identity and address proof, as requested by the campus.
  • Sibling proof, if applicable, and any additional documents specified by the school.

This is a preparation checklist based on the group’s published admission document list; the branch may request additional or different documents. Follow its current written instructions.

Fees and additional costs

A single verified, current fee table covering all three campuses was not available in the official sources reviewed. For accuracy, this guide does not publish unverified amounts or treat one campus’s fee as another campus’s fee.

Ask each branch for the full annual cost for the specific grade, including registration/admission charges, tuition or term fees, annual charges, books, uniform, meals (if offered), activities, examination charges and transport. Also ask when each instalment is due, which charges are refundable, and whether transport fees vary by route.

Parent tip: Request a dated fee circular for the current academic session and compare the complete yearly amount—not just the admission fee or one term’s payment.

Board results and achievements

Ryan Group campus pages publish achievements, news and student-life updates, but result claims should be checked against the relevant campus’s latest mandatory public disclosure and official CBSE result records. This guide does not combine campus results or present group-wide awards as if they belong to every branch.

Before admission, ask for the latest three years of Class X and (where applicable) Class XII results, including the number of students who appeared, pass percentage, subject-wise performance and the school’s method for reporting results.

Location, commute and school timings

  • Mansarovar: Sector 5, V.T. Road, Mansarovar. Check the route and morning traffic from your home.
  • Nirman Nagar: Nirman Nagar / Padmavati area. Confirm the exact entrance and bus route with the campus.
  • Jagatpura: Plot No. 2019 A, Ramachandrapura Industrial Area, Goner Road, Vidhani. Check the commute carefully if travelling from central or western Jaipur.

School timings, arrival windows, dispersal times and transport routes can vary by class and academic year. Confirm them directly with the branch. During a visit, check traffic at actual school opening and closing times, authorised pickup procedures, bus attendant arrangements and how delays are communicated to parents.

Questions to ask before you enrol

  • Is admission open for my child’s class for the current academic year, and are seats available?
  • What is the total annual fee, including all compulsory charges and transport?
  • Which subjects and streams are available in the required grade at this specific campus?
  • What are the class size, teacher availability and arrangements for extra academic support?
  • Which sports, arts, clubs, labs and leadership activities can students actually access?
  • What are the transport route, bus safety procedures, timings and emergency contact process?
  • Can I see the current mandatory public disclosure, safety certificates and latest board results?
  • What is the policy for withdrawal, transfer certificates, refunds and fee instalments?
