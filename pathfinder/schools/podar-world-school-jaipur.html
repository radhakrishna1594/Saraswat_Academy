About Podar World School Jaipur

Podar World School Jaipur is part of the Podar school group and has been operating at its Ajmer Road location since 2010. The school's published material places emphasis on academic learning, values, technology-supported teaching, creativity and activities beyond the classroom. The school group also describes a central curriculum and teacher-support system intended to maintain consistency across its schools. citeturn234625search0turn473576search0

What Parents Should Ask Before Admission

Fees

What is the complete first-year cost for my class, including one-time and optional charges?

Academics

What subjects and stream combinations are available in Classes XI–XII this session?

Transport

Is a route available for my locality, and what are the pickup/drop timings and charges?

Support

How does the school support students who need extra academic, counselling or special-education assistance?

Daily routine

How much time is given to sports, clubs, project work and other co-curricular activities?

Class experience

What is the typical class strength, homework load and assessment pattern for the child's grade?

Parent Checklist for a School Visit

  1. Check the exact route and travel time from home.
  2. Look at classrooms, labs, library and sports spaces instead of judging only from photographs.
  3. Ask for the latest class-wise fee sheet in writing.
  4. Confirm age eligibility and class vacancy.
  5. Ask how the school handles academic support and student counselling.
  6. For Classes XI–XII, ask for the current subject/stream combinations.
  7. Understand transport routes, charges and safety arrangements if transport is required.
  8. Keep copies of receipts and admission communication.
