Sanskar School, Jaipur

Sanskar School is a co-educational, English-medium senior secondary school on Sirsi Road in Jaipur. The school states that it began in April 2002 and is managed by Sri Sai Shikshan Sansthan.

About Sanskar School

According to the school's official profile and the CBSE SARAS directory, Sanskar School is a senior secondary institution affiliated with the Central Board of Secondary Education. The CBSE record lists its affiliation period as 1 April 2025 to 31 March 2030.

The school describes its approach as child-oriented, with an emphasis on academic learning, values, cooperation, self-discipline and opportunities to develop beyond the classroom.

Curriculum and academic options

The school's published curriculum covers the primary, middle, secondary and senior secondary stages. Its current programme information describes the following broad structure:

Campus and co-curricular facilities

The school's academic and public-disclosure pages describe classrooms with interactive boards and projectors, computer and subject laboratories, libraries, an auditorium, creative-arts rooms and outdoor sports spaces.

  • Computer and science laboratories, alongside specialist subject labs listed by the school.
  • Junior and senior libraries and student-resource areas.
  • Art, craft, music, dance and drama activities.
  • Sports areas that include basketball, tennis, cricket and football facilities, as described in the school's published profile.
  • Pre-primary play spaces and activity-based learning opportunities.
Visit tip: During a campus visit, ask to see the facilities relevant to your child's class, check transport arrangements and request the current school calendar and activity schedule.

Admission process for 2026–27

The school publishes admission guidance and an online registration page for the 2026–27 session. Requirements differ by entry class, and admission to classes with limited vacancies may involve an assessment or interaction.

  • For pre-primary, the school says admission is generally handled on a first-come, first-served basis, subject to its policy and seat availability.
  • For primary and middle classes, the published process may include previous report cards, an entrance assessment and an interaction with the student and parent.
  • For Classes XI–XII, confirm stream eligibility, current subject combinations and any entrance or counselling requirements directly with the school.
  • Commonly listed documents include the birth certificate, previous report card, transfer certificate where applicable, student identification and photographs. Check the current checklist before submitting documents.
