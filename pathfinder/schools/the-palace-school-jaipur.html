The Palace School, Jaipur

The Palace School is a CBSE-affiliated senior secondary school located at Jaleb Chowk within the City Palace complex in Jaipur. Its educational approach highlights holistic development, experiential learning, creativity and students' emotional, social and intellectual growth.

This independent guide brings together key details and official links to help families begin their school research. Please confirm current admissions, fees, eligibility and availability directly with the school before making decisions.

About the school

The Palace School's official website describes an education model that combines academic learning with cultural awareness, creativity, activities and whole-child development. Families should visit the campus or speak with the school to understand how its current programmes fit their child's needs.

The CBSE affiliation directory lists The Palace School at Jaleb Chowk, City Palace, Jaipur, Rajasthan 302002, as a senior secondary institution. Its published affiliation period runs from 1 April 2024 to 31 March 2029. Families can verify the latest status in the official CBSE directory.

Academics and learning approach

The school's published information describes experiential learning and a broad approach to development, including academic subjects, visual and performing arts, sports, technology and co-curricular activities.

  • Academic curriculum and subject choices appropriate to the student's class.
  • Creative activities, including visual arts, music and performance.
  • Sports and physical activities.
  • Technology-supported learning and student development programmes.
Parent tip: Ask the school for the current class-wise curriculum, subject combinations in senior secondary classes, assessment pattern, support services and details of activities available in the year you plan to enrol.

Admission and fees

Admission availability and fee schedules can change by class and academic year. We have not listed unverified fee amounts or deadlines here. Please use the school's official admission channel to request the latest information for the required class and session.

  • Confirm whether applications are open for the required class.
  • Ask for the current fee structure, one-time charges, transport charges and payment schedule in writing.
  • Confirm age criteria, documents, assessment or interaction requirements, and the admission timeline.
  • Ask about transport routes, school timings and any applicable refund policy.
