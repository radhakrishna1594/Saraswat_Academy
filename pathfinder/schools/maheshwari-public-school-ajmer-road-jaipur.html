Maheshwari Public School, Ajmer Road

Maheshwari Public School (MPS Ajmer Road) is a co-educational senior secondary school serving learners in the Bagru and Jaipur area. The school was established in 2014 and is governed by the Education Committee of the Maheshwari Samaj (Society), Jaipur.

This guide brings together information from the school's official website and the CBSE SARAS directory. Admission availability, fee amounts and class-specific details can change, so confirm the current session's information directly with the school.

About MPS Ajmer Road

The school's official About Us page says MPS Ajmer Road was established in 2014 to serve the educational needs of young learners in Jaipur and Bagru. It follows the CBSE/NCERT curriculum and is listed by CBSE as a senior secondary institution.

The school describes its educational approach as holistic, with attention to academic achievement, collaboration, self-discipline, leadership, communication and students' social and emotional development.

Important distinction: This profile is for Maheshwari Public School, Ajmer Road (Bagru), affiliation number 1730682. It is a different institution from Maheshwari Girls Public School.

Academics and learning environment

MPS Ajmer Road follows the CBSE/NCERT syllabus. Its public information highlights academic learning alongside opportunities for students to take part in creative, cultural and physical activities.

  • CBSE-aligned curriculum for the classes offered by the school.
  • Computer laboratory, library and science laboratories listed in school information.
  • Sports and physical activities as part of school life.
  • Drama, visual and performing arts, music and literary activities.
  • Activities intended to encourage leadership, teamwork, communication and self-discipline.

For the latest class-wise curriculum, subject combinations and senior-secondary stream availability, consult the school's current academic information or contact the office.
