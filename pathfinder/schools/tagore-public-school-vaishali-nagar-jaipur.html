About Tagore Public School Vaishali Nagar

Tagore Public School is an English-medium, co-educational senior-secondary school in Vaishali Nagar, Jaipur. The official CBSE SARAS record lists affiliation number 1730209, school status as Senior Secondary Level, school type as Independent, foundation year as 1992, and the managing body as Tagore Vidya Bhawan Society. The current CBSE record lists Bhavana Maini as the head of institution.

The school's official website describes the campus as being adjacent to JDA Park/Nursery Circle and says its educational approach includes futuristic learning, skill education, abacus, STEM, robotics, a Think Lab, NCC training and meditation. It also highlights solar panels and GPS-enabled student transport.

Admission 2026–27

The school currently advertises admissions for Nursery to IX and XI for the 2026–27 academic year. For Class XI, the school lists Science, Commerce and Humanities options. The official admission information states that admission is open to boys and girls and that the school follows applicable RTE directives.

📝 Registration

The school prospectus and registration form can be obtained from the school office subject to the school's registration process and applicable non-refundable registration fee.

🎂 Pre-Primary Documents

For pre-primary students, the school states that a birth certificate issued by the local governing body is required.

📄 Class I & Above

Students entering Class I and above are required to produce a Transfer Certificate from the previous school, with the school specifying DEO countersignature requirements.

✅ After Selection

The school states that fees and required certificates, photographs, PEN, APAAR ID and Aadhaar Card must be submitted within three days of admission declaration.

Admission caution: Availability, age criteria, registration deadlines and document requirements can change. Confirm the latest class-wise instructions directly from the official school resources before submitting an application.

Academics & Curriculum

Tagore Public School follows the CBSE curriculum through senior secondary level. The school's published admission information identifies Science, Commerce and Humanities for Class XI. The school also promotes technology-supported and skill-oriented learning alongside conventional academic subjects.

📚 CBSE Education

The school is affiliated with CBSE and offers schooling through the senior-secondary stage.

🧮 Abacus & Skill Learning

The school highlights abacus training for primary students and skill-oriented learning as part of its educational programme.

🤖 AI & Robotics

The school states that its AI and Robotics Lab supports machine-learning models, problem solving, decision making and prediction-oriented projects.

💡 Atal Tinkering Lab

The school describes its Atal Tinkering Lab as a project-based innovation space for students from Classes VI to XII.

Technology, Infrastructure & Student Development

☀️ Solar Energy

The school says solar panels have been installed on the rooftop as part of its environmental initiative.

🛡️ Campus Security

The school's website states that more than 100 cameras are installed across the school premises for student safety and security.

🚌 GPS Transport

GPS-enabled transport is listed among the school's facilities, with services provided on scheduled routes.

🧘 Meditation

Meditation and emotional development are included in the school's stated holistic-education approach.

🏅 NCC

NCC training is highlighted by the school as part of its wider student-development activities.

🌱 Character Development

The school's philosophy places emphasis on responsible choices, community service, emotional management, confidence and character development.

Sports, Clubs & Activities
