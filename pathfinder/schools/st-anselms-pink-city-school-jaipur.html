St. Anselm's Pink City School, Jaipur

St. Anselm's Pink City School is a co-educational senior secondary school in Malviya Nagar, Jaipur. Founded in 1987, the school is run by Gyandeep Education Society and describes its educational mission through the motto “Jyoti se Jyoti”.

This independent guide helps families begin their research into academics, facilities and admissions. Please confirm the latest class availability, fees, dates and requirements directly with the school before applying.

About the school

The school's official website identifies St. Anselm's Pink City School as a Christian minority co-educational institution managed by Gyandeep Education Society. Its published profile highlights academic learning, mentoring, personal development and a range of creative and physical activities.

The CBSE SARAS directory lists the institution as a senior secondary school with affiliation number 1730064 and school code 10451. The record shows an affiliation period from 1 April 2024 to 31 March 2029. Check the official CBSE record for the latest status.

Academics and facilities

The school's published information describes programmes across Science, Commerce and Humanities, alongside academic and co-curricular opportunities. Availability of specific subjects and activities can depend on class and academic session.

  • Senior secondary academic pathways, including Science, Commerce and Humanities as described by the school.
  • Science and computer laboratories and technology-supported classrooms.
  • Sports and physical education facilities.
  • Art, music, dance and other co-curricular activities.
Parent tip: Ask for the latest subject combinations for Classes XI–XII, the current timetable, activity options, support services and the written fee schedule for the class you are considering.
