Maharaja Sawai Man Singh Vidyalaya, Jaipur

Maharaja Sawai Man Singh Vidyalaya (MSMSV) is a long-established co-educational school in central Jaipur. Located on Sawai Ram Singh Road, the Vidyalaya combines a CBSE senior-secondary programme with a Cambridge pathway and places strong emphasis on academics, the arts, sports, leadership, community service and international exposure.

About Maharaja Sawai Man Singh Vidyalaya

MSMSV was established in 1984 by the Sawan Ram Singh Shilp Kala Mandir Society. The school was founded with an educational vision associated with Rajmata Gayatri Devi and has developed into a well-known co-educational day school in the heart of Jaipur.

The school's published material describes an approach that brings together traditional values, creativity, scientific and technological learning, cultural awareness and participation beyond the classroom. Its activities include academic clubs, arts, sports, NCC, environmental work, community service and international cultural exchange. citeturn6search0turn6search1

CBSE and Cambridge Curriculum

One of the distinctive features of MSMSV is that the school has developed an international dimension alongside its CBSE programme. Current school-related profiles and the school's Cambridge programme material describe Cambridge learning alongside the CBSE curriculum. The Cambridge programme is intended to provide an alternative international academic pathway for students and families seeking that route. citeturn8search7turn8search8

📘 CBSE Programme

The CBSE pathway runs through the senior-secondary level and includes the standard CBSE academic structure, assessments and board examinations.

The school also has a Cambridge programme with an international orientation. Exact entry points, subjects and progression should be confirmed for the current session.

Academic Approach

The school combines classroom teaching with activities that encourage communication, creativity, scientific enquiry, leadership and independent participation. Its house system and clubs give students responsibilities outside ordinary lessons, while the school's international dimension provides opportunities to connect learning with other cultures.

The school's published annual report also records the introduction of additional club, remedial and robotics periods and the use of interactive panels in classrooms. These details indicate an effort to combine conventional academic teaching with technology and enrichment. citeturn6search1

Sports and Physical Education

Sports are an important part of student life at MSMSV. The school maintains a dedicated sports programme and includes physical activity among its wider co-curricular offerings. For parents comparing schools, it is worth asking about the sports available to the child's age group, coaching arrangements, practice schedule and participation in inter-school competitions.

Activities, Clubs and Student Life

The school's activities programme is particularly broad. Students are organised into houses, and house activities include morning assembly, wall magazines, news and thought boards and student-led responsibilities.

Academic Results and Achievements

MSMSV has a long record of academic achievements. In one of its published annual reports, the school highlighted high-scoring students in both Class X and Class XII and reported city-level toppers. The same report noted Ananya Mehta's 99.8% in Class X and Nihar Mathur's 99.2% in the Class XII Science stream for the examination year covered by that report. citeturn1search1

Results from individual years should be treated as historical evidence rather than a guarantee of future performance. Parents should ask the school for the latest official board-result summary for the session they are considering.

School Achievements Beyond Board Results

The Vidyalaya has also participated in science and academic competitions. For example, students from MSMSV were selected for Rajasthan's state-level camp in Vidyarthi Vigyan Manthan, and a Class VI MSMSV student secured second rank at the Rajasthan state-level camp in 2024–25. citeturn8search23turn8search24

Such achievements are useful to parents because they show opportunities for students who enjoy science, competitions and activities beyond the regular examination system.

Parent Checklist for a School Visit

  1. Check the exact class vacancy and eligibility.
  2. Ask for the latest fee sheet and all compulsory charges.
  3. Visit classrooms, laboratories, library and sports facilities.
  4. Ask about the Cambridge pathway if you are considering an international curriculum.
  5. For Classes XI–XII, collect the current subject-combination list.
  6. Ask about transport routes, safety and travel time.
  7. Understand the school's assessment and remedial-support approach.
  8. Ask how students participate in clubs, sports and competitions.
  9. Keep copies of the application, fee receipts and admission communication.
