About Teoler High School

Teoler High School is a co-educational senior secondary school on Maharana Pratap Marg, Kunda Sirsi Road, opposite Manglam Grand Residency in Jaipur. The school was founded in 2014 and is affiliated with CBSE. The current CBSE record lists the affiliation period from 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2031.

The school describes its teaching approach around critical, creative and applied learning. It also places considerable emphasis on mentorship, classroom support and preparation for students who want to pursue competitive examinations along with their regular school studies.

For parents: A school should be judged by how well it fits your child's needs, learning style, commute, budget and future plans. A campus visit and a conversation with the school are still important before admission.

Admission Process

Teoler's published admission procedure says registration forms for new-session admissions are made available online each year from 21 November. Admission to Pre-primary and Grade I is through a lottery system followed by an offline interaction/verification process. For Classes II to XII, the school uses an online or offline assessment, with a second round possible when required.

Classes, Curriculum and Senior Secondary

Teoler High School runs up to the senior secondary level. Public academic information lists Science and Commerce pathways at senior secondary level, while school information also refers to integrated preparation for competitive examinations. Exact Class XI subject combinations should be confirmed with the school for the relevant session.

Regular school education

  • CBSE curriculum
  • Pre-primary through senior secondary
  • English-medium instruction

Integrated preparation

The school publishes integrated programmes connected with JEE, NEET, IPMAT/UG management, CLAT/BCA, CUET and foundation-level preparation. These programmes may involve additional classes and additional fees, so parents should ask for the current course plan and fee before opting in.

Facilities and Campus

The school's mandatory disclosure lists a campus area of 12,044 square metres, with 87 large classrooms, 7 medium classrooms and 6 small classrooms. It also lists internet facilities and separate sanitation facilities.

Teoler's own website describes classrooms, a library, laboratories, computer facilities and sports infrastructure. The school also states that its Physics, Chemistry and Biology laboratories are designed for CBSE practical work, with capacity for students to perform experiments under teacher and laboratory-assistant supervision.

Technology and safety

The school says that its campus is covered by CCTV cameras and that school buses are GPS enabled. Its safety information also describes fire-safety, water and sanitation certifications, security guards and supervised student movement during sports, trips and other activities.

Other Teoler Schools and Centres in Jaipur

Teoler Unbounded

Teoler Unbounded is a separate Cambridge-oriented school under the Teoler group. The school says it was brought into the group in 2019–20 and offers Cambridge qualifications. Its published address is Maharana Pratap Marg, Sirsi Kunda Road, Jaipur.

Teoler Pre School

Teoler also operates preschool centres. Its official pages list centres in Vaishali Nagar, Sirsi and Nirman Nagar. The Vaishali Nagar centre's published 2026–27 fee page lists Play Group through HKG under its preschool programme.

These are separate programmes or campuses. Parents should check the exact campus, curriculum, age group, admission rules and fee structure before applying.

What Parents Should Check Before Admission

  1. Ask for the latest fee circular for the exact class and session.
  2. Confirm whether transport is available for your locality and ask for the complete transport fee.
  3. For Class XI, ask for the exact subject combinations and timetable.
  4. If choosing an integrated JEE, NEET or management programme, ask how many additional hours are involved each week.
  5. Ask about class strength, teacher availability and academic support for students who need extra help.
  6. Visit the laboratories, library, sports areas and classrooms rather than relying only on photographs.
  7. Read the withdrawal, refund and Transfer Certificate rules before paying admission charges.
