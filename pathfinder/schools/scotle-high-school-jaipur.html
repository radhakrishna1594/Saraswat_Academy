SCOTLE High School, Jaipur

SCOTLE High School is a CBSE-affiliated senior secondary school at Adinath Nagar on Sirsi Road, Jaipur. The school was founded in 2019 and offers programmes from the early years through senior secondary education, with Foundation and School Integrated Programmes for older students.

About the School

SCOTLE High School is an independent, CBSE-affiliated school in Jaipur. CBSE records list the school as a senior secondary institution, with affiliation number 1731084, school code 11571, foundation year 2019 and the Adinath Nagar, Sirsi Road address.

The school describes its approach around personalised learning, conceptual understanding, critical thinking, moral values and opportunities for students to develop academic, creative, sporting and leadership skills.

Admission 2026–27

SCOTLE's official website states that admissions for the 2026–27 session are open. Its published admission information covers Kindergarten through Middle School, the Grade 9 Foundation Programme and Grade 11 Science and Commerce SIP programmes.

School Entry

Admissions are handled through the school's application and interaction process. Class-wise availability and eligibility should be confirmed with the admissions office.

Grade 9 Foundation

The Foundation Programme focuses on strengthening Mathematics and Science concepts and preparing students for the senior-secondary stage.

Grade 11 Science

The Science SIP combines the CBSE curriculum with structured preparation options for examinations such as JEE, NEET, NDA, BITSAT and CUET. Current subject combinations should be confirmed with the school.

Grade 11 Commerce

The Commerce SIP covers board preparation with pathways that may include Mathematics, Informatics Practices, Psychology and Physical Education, along with preparation for examinations such as CUET, IPMAT and CA Foundation.

For the current application form, class-wise eligibility, document checklist, seat availability and admission-stage requirements, contact the school directly or use the official application link on the SCOTLE website.

Academics and Curriculum

SCOTLE follows the CBSE curriculum across its school stages. The school describes a progression from early learning and primary education to the Grade 9–10 Foundation Programme and specialised senior-secondary Science and Commerce pathways.

Kindergarten

The early-years programme uses play, activities, storytelling and guided learning to build foundational skills and curiosity.

Primary School

Primary learning includes core subjects, language development, activity-based work and the school's Jodo Gyan approach to mathematics.

Middle School

Students develop stronger subject foundations across Mathematics, Science, Social Studies, languages and Information Technology, with arts and physical education alongside academics.

High School

Grades 9 and 10 follow the Foundation Programme, while Grades 11 and 12 provide Science and Commerce SIP pathways with board and entrance-exam preparation options.

Senior Secondary Streams

Science

SCOTLE's Science pathway includes Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics or Biology, with structured preparation options for JEE, NEET, NDA, CUET and other examinations. Informatics Practices and Physical Education are listed among elective possibilities.

Commerce

The Commerce pathway covers Accountancy, Business Studies and Economics, with options including Mathematics, Informatics Practices, Psychology and Physical Education. The school also describes preparation pathways for CUET, IPMAT and CA Foundation.

Campus and Facilities

The school publishes a range of facilities designed for classroom learning, practical work, technology, reading, arts and sports.

Smart Classrooms

Digital classrooms and multimedia-supported teaching are part of the school's published infrastructure.

Science, STEM and Robotics

The school highlights science laboratories, a STEM lab, makerspace, robotics and design-thinking activities for hands-on learning.

Library

The school lists a library with print and digital learning resources to support reading, research and independent study.

Creative Spaces

Art, drama, music and dance activities provide opportunities for creative expression and stage performance.

Sports

Published sports facilities and activities include basketball, football, volleyball, squash, chess and other indoor and outdoor activities.

Transport and Security

The school states that its buses use GPS and CCTV, while campus safety information includes controlled entry and CCTV surveillance.

Sports and Co-curricular Activities

Sports and co-curricular activities form a regular part of student life at SCOTLE. The school publishes activities including basketball, football, volleyball, squash, chess, dance, music, art and culture and martial arts. Annual events such as INVICTUS provide opportunities for athletics and team competitions.

The school also organises STEM activities, debates, public-speaking programmes, cultural events, Olympiad participation and leadership opportunities.

Student Development and Partnerships

SCOTLE publishes several programmes and partnerships connected with student learning and development. These include Jodo Gyan for activity-based mathematics, Mindsprings for English-language education, RockSport Adventures for outdoor and leadership activities, India Spelling Bee and Six Brick Sessions with Sunday Bricks.

The school also describes a Career Counselling Cell that begins guidance from Grade 8 and continues into the senior grades, including aptitude assessment and stream-related guidance.
