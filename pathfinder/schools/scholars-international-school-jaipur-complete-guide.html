2. Learning approach and academics

The school describes its approach as joyful and experiential, encouraging children to participate and develop academic, social and emotional skills. It highlights self-learning materials, smart classrooms and activities beyond textbook learning. These are the school’s stated aims; parents should see how they are applied in their child’s actual class and timetable.

During a school visit, ask the academic coordinator about:

  • Class strength and the number of teachers assigned to each grade.
  • How Mathematics, Science, languages and other subjects are taught and assessed.
  • Homework expectations and the frequency of tests and parent updates.
  • Support for students who need remedial teaching or additional challenge.
  • Use of self-learning materials, digital boards and project-based activities.
  • Availability of subject combinations in Classes XI and XII for the session you need.

3. Admissions and entry classes

The school’s website has displayed an admission notice for the 2025–26 session for Nursery to Class IX and Class XI in Science, Commerce and Arts. That notice is now historical; it should not be treated as confirmation that applications are open for the current session. Contact the school directly for current 2026–27 vacancies, the admission window and classes accepting applications.

Questions to ask the admission office

  • Is admission available for my child’s class and age in the current session?
  • What are the age criteria and cut-off date for the entry class?
  • Is an interaction, entrance assessment or previous report card required?
  • Which subject streams and combinations are available in Classes XI and XII?
  • What is the deadline, registration fee and refund policy?
  • Are transport seats available for our locality?

Documents parents may need

  • Child’s birth certificate and recent passport-size photographs.
  • Previous school report card, where applicable.
  • Transfer certificate and other school records, if required for the class.
  • Parent/guardian identification and address proof as requested.
  • Any additional documents specified by the school for the intended class.

Use the official school website or contact page to confirm the latest process. Do not pay a fee to an unofficial intermediary.

7. Sports, clubs and student life

The school says it organises cultural events and activities such as recitation, dance, songs, debates, drawing, art and craft workshops. Its sports page lists several outdoor and indoor games. These opportunities can help children build confidence, teamwork and discipline when participation is regular and inclusive.

Parents should ask whether extracurricular activities are part of the weekly timetable or mainly occasional events, whether students can choose activities, and whether coaching or competitions incur additional charges. If a child has a particular interest—such as football, cricket, music, drawing or public speaking—ask about the actual time and support available for it.

8. Location and transport

The school is located at Shree Narayan Campus, Niwaru Link Road, Kalwar Road, Govindpura/Hathoj, Jaipur 302012. Before admission, test the commute from your home during usual school travel hours; traffic and pickup sequence can make the actual journey longer than the map estimate.

  • Does the school bus cover your exact colony or street?
  • What are the expected pickup and drop-off times?
  • Who supervises younger students while boarding and leaving the bus?
  • How are parents informed about delays, route changes or emergencies?
  • What is the transport fee and refund policy if the route changes?

Confirm the campus gate and map location through the official contact page before travelling.

9. Safety, wellbeing and parent communication

The school’s mandatory disclosure page lists documents related to building safety, fire safety, water and sanitation. Parents should review the current documents and ask about day-to-day student protection—not just the presence of a certificate.

  • How are visitors checked before entering the campus?
  • What is the student handover process at the gate and on school transport?
  • Who is the designated contact for a child-safety concern or grievance?
  • How does the school handle bullying, peer conflict and repeated behavioural concerns?
  • What first-aid and emergency-response arrangements are in place?
  • How are parents informed about attendance, academic progress and important incidents?

The school disclosure also lists a counsellor and a special educator. Confirm the current staff availability, how students can access support and how parents can request a confidential discussion.

10. Parent checklist before admission

  1. Confirm the school’s exact address and CBSE affiliation number 1730905.
  2. Ask whether seats are available in the required class for the current academic session.
  3. Get the complete fee circular, including transport and optional activity charges.
  4. Ask about class strength, teacher allocation and remedial support.
  5. Visit the science and computer labs, library, playground and washrooms.
  6. Confirm bus routes, travel time, supervision and emergency communication.
  7. Review the latest affiliation, building-safety and fire-safety documents.
  8. Ask for the withdrawal, fee-adjustment and refund policy in writing.
  9. Discuss how the school reports progress and responds to parent concerns.
  10. Keep copies of forms, fee receipts and important school communication.
