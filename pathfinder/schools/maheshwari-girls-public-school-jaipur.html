Maheshwari Girls Public School, Jaipur

Maheshwari Girls Public School (MGPS) is a senior secondary girls' school in Sector 1, Vidyadhar Nagar, Jaipur. The school follows the CBSE and NCERT curriculum from Class I to XII and places emphasis on academics, co-curricular activities, sports, technology and the development of confident, capable students.

About MGPS Jaipur

Maheshwari Girls Public School is a senior secondary school affiliated with the Central Board of Secondary Education. The school's official profile describes MGPS as an institution combining contemporary education with cultural values and identifies its motto as “making girls powerful & serene.”

The current CBSE record lists the school as Maheshwari Girls Pub School, affiliation number 1730172, school code 10517, and senior secondary level. It records 2001 as the foundation year and 1 April 2005 as the date of first opening. The current CBSE record names Ms. Sarika Gaur as Principal. citeturn0search7

Registration

The admission instructions state that the registration form must be completed carefully online. For the published 2026–27 process, the registration fee is ₹500 plus applicable payment gateway charges and is non-refundable.

Important point

Submitting the online registration form does not by itself confirm admission. Parents should follow the school's instructions and wait for the school's admission communication.

Documents mentioned by the school

  • Two recent passport-size photographs of the child
  • Government-issued birth certificate
  • Aadhaar Card of the candidate
  • PAN Card or Aadhaar Card of both parents
  • Proof of residence
  • Proof of annual income
  • Blood group report of the student
  • Mark sheet of the last class attended
  • Relevant category, minority or disability certificate, where applicable

Academics and Subjects

MGPS follows the CBSE/NCERT framework and offers education from Class I through Class XII. The school publishes a detailed class-wise curriculum covering academic subjects as well as co-scholastic activities. citeturn7view1

Classes I–V

English, Hindi, Mathematics, Science/Environmental Studies, Social Studies, Computer Science and value-based learning are included, with activities such as art, music, dance, yoga, skating and Taekwondo.

Classes VI–VIII

English, Hindi, Sanskrit, Science & Technology, Mathematics, Computer Science and Social Science are taught. Artificial Intelligence and Financial Literacy are also offered as skill subjects.

Classes IX–X

The published curriculum includes English, Hindi or Sanskrit, Science & Technology, Mathematics, Social Science, Artificial Intelligence and an introduction to financial markets.

Classes XI–XII

Students can choose elective combinations in Science, Commerce and Humanities. Subjects listed by the school include Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Mathematics, Applied Mathematics, Information Practices, Economics, Psychology, Accountancy, Business Studies, History, Geography and Political Science.
