About Maheshwari Girls Public School

Maheshwari Girls Public School (MGPS) is a girls' senior secondary school affiliated with the Central Board of Secondary Education. The school states that it follows the NCERT/CBSE syllabi and offers education from Class I to Class XII. Its published philosophy places emphasis on academic learning together with social, emotional and physical development.

The school's official mandatory disclosure records affiliation number 1730172, school code 10517, the address at Sector-1, Vidhyadhar Nagar, Jaipur and Ms. Sarika Gaur as principal. The official disclosure also lists the school's infrastructure and contact information.

2026–27 Admission

For the 2026–27 session, the school's admission page publishes age eligibility for Nursery, KG, Prep and Class I. Registration forms are to be submitted online through the school's website. The school states that incomplete or incorrect applications may be rejected and that successful form submission is followed by an email confirmation.

Registration and documents

  • Online registration is completed through the school's website.
  • The registration fee is ₹1,000 plus applicable payment gateway charges and is non-refundable.
  • Parents are instructed to keep a printout of the submitted form and registration fee receipt.
  • Documents listed by the school include birth certificate, residence proof, recent photographs, blood-group report, previous school records and Aadhaar copies for parents and child.
  • Where applicable, the school also asks for minority, caste or disability certificates.
  • The school states that the original Transfer Certificate and final examination mark sheet must be deposited by the stated deadline for provisional admission.
Admission check: Dates, class availability and document requirements can change between sessions. Use the current admission notice on the official MGPS website before completing an application.

Academics and Curriculum

MGPS describes itself as a Senior Secondary School affiliated with CBSE and says it follows NCERT/CBSE syllabi from Class I to XII. The school's current academic material separately lists Class XI and XII Science, Commerce and Humanities material.

📚 School Education

The academic programme combines classroom teaching with experiential activities, clubs, workshops, competitions and opportunities for students to develop communication, problem-solving and collaboration skills.

🎓 Senior Secondary

Students in the senior classes can follow Science, Commerce or Humanities pathways as published by the school. Subject combinations and availability should be checked for the required session.

Campus and Infrastructure

MGPS's mandatory disclosure reports a campus area of 7,980 square metres, 91 classrooms, nine laboratories, a 147.56-square-metre library and internet facility. The disclosure also records 108 girls' toilets, five boys' toilets and four CWSN toilets.

The school's infrastructure page describes Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Biotechnology, Computer, English Language and Humanities laboratories; three computer labs; a library; auditorium; audio-visual centre; resource room; art, music and dance rooms; smart classrooms; a health room; canteen; book shop and uniform corner. It also mentions an elevator and public-address system.

💻 Technology & Labs

Three computer labs and multiple subject laboratories are described by the school, along with internet connectivity and smart classrooms.

📖 Library & Learning Spaces

The school describes a well-stocked library supported by dedicated spaces for audio-visual learning, resource support, art, music and dance.

🏫 Campus Facilities

The published infrastructure information includes an auditorium, sports room, health room, canteen and other day-to-day student facilities.

🌱 Campus Environment

The school's leadership material describes maintained gardens, solar panels and rainwater harvesting as part of the campus environment.

Sports and Activities

The school provides a broad range of indoor and outdoor activities. Its physical-education page lists handball, basketball, kabaddi, athletics, badminton, chess and carrom, while yoga and taekwondo are also part of the programme. The infrastructure page additionally mentions skating, aerobics and band facilities.

MGPS also publishes information about NCC, Guide & Bulbul, school band, dance and music, art and craft, inter-house events, school clubs, community outreach and inter-school competitions.

House System

The school follows a four-house system for academic, cultural and outdoor activities: Saraswati House, Durga House, Gargi House and Raj Rani Meera House. The student council includes the Head Girl, Deputy Head Girl, Vice-Captains and House Captains, with council members selected through voting.
