Neerja Modi School, Jaipur

Neerja Modi School (NMS) is a well-known school group in Jaipur. This guide separates the campus information from those important regulatory updates so families can make enquiries with the right questions in hand.

CBSE affiliation and current admission position

This section is deliberately prominent because admission decisions depend on the school's current legal and regulatory status, not only on the school's marketing page.

What the published record says

  • 30 December 2025: CBSE issued an order concerning the Mansarovar school's affiliation after reviewing child-safety and compliance matters. The order and subsequent proceedings are available through CBSE's official website.
  • 23 February 2026: media reports on CBSE's modified order said affiliation was restored to secondary level for Classes IX–X, while senior-secondary affiliation for Classes XI–XII remained withdrawn for two academic years. Existing students were addressed separately in the order.
  • March 2026: reports described interim relief granted by the Rajasthan High Court in the school's challenge to the CBSE order. An interim stay should not be mistaken for a final judgment or a blanket confirmation that every grade can accept new students.
  • 18 March 2026: reports on a separate Rajasthan education department order said new admissions to Class XI were barred for sessions 2026–27 and 2027–28, and Class XII admissions were barred for 2027–28. The report also said an officer was appointed to help affected families with transitions.

These developments involve different authorities and orders. This guide is not a legal opinion and does not claim to resolve any conflict between them. Before applying, ask for the latest written clarification that specifically covers the child's target class, academic session, board registration and the effect of any court order.

Questions to ask in writing

  • Is the requested class authorised to admit new students for the target session?
  • What is the latest applicable CBSE order number and date?
  • Has the Rajasthan education department issued any subsequent modification or withdrawal?
  • For Classes IX–X, what are the current affiliation and board-registration arrangements?
  • For Classes XI–XII, what written confirmation exists about admission, promotion, subject combinations and board examinations?
  • What arrangements are available for a transfer if the family decides to move?

Official source: CBSE order dated 30 December 2025 (PDF). Also consult the live CBSE SARAS school record. Media reports linked in the sources section summarise later developments but should not replace the actual orders.

Child safety, student wellbeing and parent questions

The CBSE action and subsequent public reporting followed the death of a nine-year-old student at the Mansarovar campus in November 2025. CBSE's published order refers to concerns around compliance with child-safety requirements. This is a serious matter and should be approached respectfully, without speculation about individual children or families.

When considering any school, parents have every reason to ask clear questions about safeguarding, supervision, anti-bullying procedures, counselling, complaint handling and how concerns are escalated. Ask for the school's current written policies and how they are implemented in day-to-day practice—not only whether a committee or policy exists on paper.

Safety and wellbeing checklist for a school visit

  • Who is the designated child-protection / safeguarding contact?
  • How can a student report bullying or unsafe behaviour confidentially?
  • How are complaints recorded, investigated and communicated to parents?
  • What supervision is provided in corridors, stairways, playgrounds and other shared areas?
  • What counselling and mental-health support is available, and how can students access it?
  • How are emergencies handled, and how are parents notified?
  • Can the school explain its current compliance with CBSE safety requirements and the latest official directions?

Parents should feel comfortable asking these questions in a calm, direct manner. If an answer is unclear, request the relevant policy or written clarification before proceeding.

Where is Neerja Modi School located?

What is the CBSE affiliation number for the Mansarovar campus?

Can new students join Class XI in 2026–27?

Reports on a Rajasthan education department order dated 18 March 2026 state that new Class XI admissions were barred for sessions 2026–27 and 2027–28. Confirm whether any subsequent court or government order has changed this before taking action.

Is senior-secondary affiliation currently available?

CBSE's modified order reported in February 2026 withdrew senior-secondary affiliation for Classes XI–XII for two academic years, while subsequent court proceedings reportedly granted interim relief.
