About Mayoor School Jaipur

Mayoor School Jaipur is a senior-secondary co-educational school established by the Global Institute of Technology Society. The school states that it works in collaboration with the Mayo College General Council, Ajmer. It offers schooling from the early years through Grade XII, with day-boarding and hostel facilities.

The CBSE affiliation record lists the school's affiliation number as 1731182, school code 11713, foundation year 2022, and senior-secondary status. The CBSE record identifies Aji Kumar K.N. as the head of institution.

Admission 2026–27

The school's official admission page currently lists admissions for the 2026–27 academic year from Nursery/Early Years through Grade IX and Grade XI. The published process has three main stages.

1. Registration

Submit the admission waitlist/enquiry form. The admission team then coordinates with the family.

2. Application

Complete the detailed admission form shared through the registered email, pay the applicable form fee and complete the online steps.

3. Verification

Submit the required documents for verification. Provisional admission is subject to the school's admission process and approval.

Admission availability is class-specific. Confirm the current vacancy, age requirements, documents and dates with the school's admission office before applying.

Academics and Curriculum

Mayoor School Jaipur describes its academic approach as experiential and outcome-driven. Its website describes CBSE-aligned academics alongside a Cambridge pathway, with learning designed around conceptual understanding, practical application and student participation.

Early Years

The school's early-years programme covers Early Years I, II and III and focuses on building a foundation for learning through age-appropriate experiences.

Primary & Middle School

Grades I–VIII are supported by integrated learning, clubs, practical activities, technology and subject-based learning.

Secondary

Grades IX–X follow the senior-school academic programme, with opportunities for STEM, sports, arts and other enrichment activities.

Senior Secondary

Grades XI–XII form the senior-secondary stage. Families should confirm current subject combinations and pathway availability directly with the school.

Campus and Facilities

The school lists a range of academic, technology, sports and student-support facilities. Its published infrastructure information records a campus area of approximately 8,719 square metres and six laboratories, in addition to classrooms and other facilities.

STEM & Robotics

The STEM and Robotics Lab provides workstations, laptops and robotics kits for practical projects and experimentation.

Laboratories

The school lists science, mathematics, social science, psychology and computer-related learning facilities.

Library

The school maintains a library and promotes reading and independent learning as part of its academic programme.

Auditoriums

The school states that it has four auditoriums with seating capacities of approximately 600, 300, 200 and 100.

Sports

Published facilities include cricket, basketball, volleyball, swimming, athletics and indoor games, along with activities such as tennis, squash, skating, table tennis, martial arts and yoga.

Day Boarding

The day-boarding programme includes structured study time, sports, meals and recreational or innovative activities.

Sports and Student Activities

Sports and co-scholastic programmes form a substantial part of the school's published programme. Activities include cricket, football, basketball, swimming, tennis, martial arts, yoga, chess, table tennis, skating and squash. The school also lists clubs covering STEM, visual and performing arts, heritage and environment, theatre and literature, cooking and hospitality, and general knowledge.

Safety, Transport and Boarding

The school states that its campus uses CCTV surveillance, trained security personnel, secure entry procedures and a medical room. Its transport information includes air-conditioned buses with ABS, GPS and fire-fighting equipment, along with experienced drivers.

Mayoor School Jaipur also offers day-boarding and hostel facilities. Parents considering residential accommodation should confirm current hostel availability, boarding fees, age/class eligibility, room arrangements and supervision directly with the school.

School Timings

The school's FAQ lists regular school hours as 9:00 AM to 3:00 PM. Timings for transport, day-boarding, clubs and special activities may differ.
