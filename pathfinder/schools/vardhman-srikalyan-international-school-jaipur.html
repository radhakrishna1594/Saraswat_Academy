About the School

Vardhman Srikalyan International School (VSIS) is a co-educational CBSE senior secondary school at Hathoj on Kalwar Road in Jaipur. CBSE records show that the school was founded in 2006 and has affiliation number 1730328, with its current listed affiliation period running from 1 April 2023 to 31 March 2028.

The school describes its approach as a combination of academic learning, values, experiential learning and co-curricular opportunities. Its published programme includes academics from the early years through Class XII, along with activities in sports, music, art, drama and other areas.

Important: This guide is for educational reference. Admission rules, fees, subjects, transport charges and availability can change. Parents should confirm the current details with the school before making a final decision.

Admission Information

The school's current admissions page describes an admission process beginning with the prospectus and registration form, followed by submission of the completed form and a student-parent interaction with the Principal. The school states that admissions are considered for Play Group through Class IX and Class XI, subject to its current availability and admission policy.

Documents generally requested

  • Student photographs
  • Student Aadhaar card
  • Original birth certificate with a self-attested copy
  • Current residence proof
  • Transfer Certificate, where applicable
  • Previous class result, where applicable
  • Relevant certificate for weaker-section/disadvantaged-group applicants, where applicable

The school's online registration form currently displays a registration charge of ₹500. Parents should verify the amount and the complete admission procedure for the session in which they are applying.

Fees: What Parents Should Know

A current official 2026–27 fee notice for the Hathoj campus was not found on the school's public website during preparation of this guide. For that reason, Saraswat Academy does not present a third-party estimate as the school's official fee structure.

Public school-directory listings currently show indicative figures that vary by class. For example, one current listing estimates recurring annual fees of about ₹33,000 for Nursery, ₹42,000 for Classes 1–5, ₹51,000 for Classes 6–8, ₹59,000 for Classes 9–10 and ₹70,000 for Class 11 Science, with one-time registration/admission charges shown separately. These are estimates, not an official school fee notice.

Before admission, ask the school for the latest written fee circular and confirm tuition/composite fee, admission fee, transport, books, uniform, activities, examination charges and any refundable or non-refundable deposits separately.

Academics and Senior Secondary

The school follows the CBSE curriculum and presents its teaching approach around core academic learning, critical thinking, problem-solving, creativity and experiential learning.

Senior-secondary pathways

Public school information lists Science, Commerce and Arts/Humanities pathways, with the school also describing foundation support for areas such as IIT, JEE and NEET. Exact subject combinations should be confirmed directly for the relevant session.

Facilities and Learning Environment

The school's published information highlights technology-enabled and air-conditioned classrooms, laboratories, a STEM-focused learning space, activity areas, an auditorium, sports infrastructure and spaces for indoor and outdoor activities.

Its CBSE-related documentation also refers to laboratory infrastructure, a library and facilities required for a senior-secondary school. The school website describes hands-on learning and opportunities for students to explore interests beyond regular classroom study.

The Vardhman Group website also lists a separate Mansarovar Branch, Vardhman International School, at Sector 3, Main Shipra Path, Mansarovar, Jaipur – 302020. It has a separate CBSE affiliation number, 1730889, so parents should not treat it as the same school campus as Vardhman Srikalyan International School.

This distinction matters when comparing fees, admissions, facilities, school code and affiliation details.

Questions Parents Should Ask Before Admission

  1. What is the complete fee payable for the selected class for the current session?
  2. Are transport, books, uniform and activities charged separately?
  3. Which subjects and combinations are available in Class XI?
  4. What is the school transport route and pickup/drop timing for our locality?
  5. What are the class strength and teacher-student ratio for the child's grade?
  6. What support is available for students who need additional academic help?
  7. What are the rules for fee refunds, withdrawal and transfer certificates?
