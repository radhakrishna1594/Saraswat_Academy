About PRG World School Jaipur

PRG World School was founded in 2015 and is managed by Shri Saraswati Punyarth Trust. The CBSE SARAS record classifies it as an independent senior-secondary school in Jaipur, with affiliation no. 1730880. The CBSE record currently lists Ritu Chaturvedi as the head of institution.

The school's own website says its aim is to encourage lifelong learning and help students become responsible members of society. It highlights creativity, empathy, academic development, self-discipline, communication, independence, problem-solving, teamwork and respect as important elements of school life.

Why Parents May Consider PRG World School

📚 Academic Focus

The school combines CBSE academics with an emphasis on learning outcomes, literacy, numeracy, preparation for assessments and overall academic development.

🌱 Values & Character

The school's stated philosophy gives importance to empathy, inclusion, respect, honesty, responsibility and discipline.

💡 Creativity & Expression

The school encourages creativity and provides learning opportunities beyond textbook study through activities, arts and student participation.

🤝 Student Support

CBSE disclosure information includes a special educator and a counsellor/wellness teacher, supporting a broader student-development approach.

Admission to PRG World School

The school's official website provides an online admission/re-admission application form. The form asks for academic-session and class details, student information, parent/guardian information, address details and previous-school information. It also includes a choice for whether transport is required.

1. Check Class Availability

Start by confirming whether admission is available in the required class for the relevant session.

2. Submit Application

Use the school's current admission form or registration route and enter the student's and parent's details accurately.

3. Prepare Documents

Keep the child's birth/date-of-birth proof, photographs, previous-school records and other class-specific documents ready. The exact checklist should be confirmed with the school.

4. Complete School Formalities

Follow the school's instructions for assessment or interaction, document verification and payment after admission is offered.

Important: The school's admission form itself states that online application is subject to approval by the school. Class-wise eligibility, vacancies, age criteria and the final document list should therefore be verified before submission.

Academics and Curriculum

PRG World School follows the CBSE curriculum. Its curriculum page describes a learning model that integrates research and hands-on experiences, supports freedom of expression and creativity, and seeks to tailor instruction to students' needs, interests and abilities. It also identifies skills such as time management, literacy, effective learning, assessment preparation, public speaking and debating.

Pre-Primary / Beginners

The school describes its Beginners wing as the pre-primary stage, with attention to motor skills, fine-motor development, habits, manners, physical activity, curiosity and problem-solving.

Primary & Middle Years

Students progress through CBSE subject learning supported by classroom activities, practical experiences and co-curricular opportunities.

Secondary Stage

Classes IX and X form the secondary stage, where academic foundations become increasingly important for board assessment and future stream choices.

Senior Secondary

The school is classified by CBSE as senior secondary. Students and parents entering Classes XI–XII should confirm the latest subject combinations and stream availability for the relevant session.

How to Apply: Parent Checklist

  1. Identify the class and academic session for which admission is required.
  2. Read the school's latest admission instructions and verify vacancy.
  3. Prepare birth/date-of-birth proof, previous-school records, photographs and other documents requested for the class.
  4. Complete the official application accurately.
  5. Ask for the complete first-year cost, not only tuition.
  6. Clarify transport route availability and additional charges if transport is required.
  7. Understand the assessment/interaction process, where applicable.
  8. Visit the campus before making the final decision when practical.
  9. Keep copies of submitted documents, receipts and admission communication.
