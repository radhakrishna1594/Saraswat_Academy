Dharav High School, Jaipur

Dharav High School is a CBSE-affiliated co-educational school in Sector 6, Vidhyadhar Nagar, Jaipur. The school’s published approach combines academic learning with values, scientific temperament, creativity, sports and student activities.

Academics and curriculum

The school describes its educational approach as supporting rational thought, compassion, scientific temperament, creativity and ethical values. Its website provides separate information for pedagogy, examinations, school strength, annual planners, books and class teachers.

  • Classes: The official 2027–28 admission notice lists Pre-Nursery to Class IX and Class XI. Ask the school about the current availability of each grade and the continuation pathway into Class XII.
  • Senior secondary: Since Class XI admissions are advertised, request the latest subject-combination list for Science, Commerce or Humanities (as applicable) and confirm which combinations are actually running.
  • Learning resources: The official site lists smart classes, junior and senior laboratories, a library, art and music, and a published pedagogy section.
  • Student support: Ask about class sizes, remedial support, counselling, special educational needs, assessment schedules and parent-teacher communication.

Facilities, sports and student life

According to the school’s infrastructure information, facilities and activities include:

  • Amphitheatre: An open-air theatre used for activities such as dance, drama, singing, elocution and school functions.
  • Sports and games: The school lists cricket, football, volleyball, basketball, handball and karate, along with indoor games including table tennis, chess and carrom. Confirm which activities are available by grade and season.
  • Junior and senior laboratories: Listed on the official infrastructure menu. Ask which subject labs students use and how often practical sessions are scheduled.
  • Smart classes and library: Both are listed as school facilities. Parents can ask about digital-learning use, library periods and book-lending rules.
  • Art and music: Listed as dedicated areas of school life.
  • Cafeteria (Tuck Inn): The school describes a cafeteria serving snacks and meals. Ask for the current menu, allergy arrangements and charges.
  • Infirmary: The school states that it has basic medical facilities, a doctor and nurse, routine health checks and emergency procedures. Parents should verify the current arrangements during a visit.
  • Transport: Transport information is published on the official website. Confirm route availability, bus charges, pickup timings, supervision and safety protocols for your locality.

  • Two recent passport-size photographs of the child.
  • Child’s municipal birth certificate.
  • Child’s Aadhaar or passport, as accepted by the school.
  • Report card of the previous class passed.
