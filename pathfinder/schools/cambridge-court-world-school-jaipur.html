Cambridge Court World School, Jaipur

Cambridge Court World School is a CBSE-affiliated senior secondary school at Sector-3, Shipra Path, Varun Path, Mansarovar, Jaipur. The school began in 2016 and describes its model as day boarding, combining classroom learning with activities, hobbies, sports and life-skills programmes.

About the School

Cambridge Court World School was established in 2016 and is affiliated with the Central Board of Secondary Education. CBSE records identify it as an independent senior secondary school with affiliation number 1730867.

The school describes itself as a day boarding school. Its published programme combines academics with sports, hobbies, arts, technology, life-skills development and other co-curricular activities. The school states that its students are known as “Cambridgians”.

Academics and Curriculum

Cambridge Court World School follows the CBSE curriculum from the early grades through senior secondary. The school emphasises conceptual learning, self-learning, analytical thinking, life skills and technology-supported education.

Primary and Middle School

Students develop foundations in core subjects alongside language, technology, arts, sports and activity-based learning.

Secondary School

Classes IX and X follow the CBSE secondary curriculum, supported by laboratory work, technology and co-curricular activities.

Senior Secondary

The school offers senior-secondary education. Current subject combinations and availability should be confirmed for the relevant session.

Learning Approach

The school describes a student-centred approach with self-learning, creativity, analytical thinking, emotional intelligence and technology integration.

Day Boarding Programme

Day boarding is a central part of the school's model. The school states that students in Grades 2–7 have day-boarding hours from 9:00 AM to 4:30 PM, while Grades 8–12 are listed as day scholars from 8:00 AM to 2:30 PM. The school also describes hobby classes and structured revision as part of the extended school day.

Published hobby and activity options include volleyball, skating, synthesizer, western and classical dance, chess, theatre, martial arts, art and craft, guitar and tabla.

Sports and Co-curricular Activities

The school currently advertises more than 75 extracurricular activities. Its published programme includes sports, performing arts, clubs, technology activities, leadership opportunities and creative programmes.

Sports information on the school's website includes more than 20 sports, with activities such as archery, chess, karate, boxing and other indoor and outdoor sports. The school also describes Ideathons, Hackathons, TED-Ed Club, interdisciplinary clubs, MUN and student leadership activities.

STEAM and Creative Learning

Cambridge Court World School describes STEAM education as an important part of its creative-learning approach. Students are encouraged to work on design and problem-solving tasks involving Science, Technology, Engineering, Arts and Mathematics.

The school also lists activities in martial arts, dance, painting, football, skating and other creative and physical skills.
