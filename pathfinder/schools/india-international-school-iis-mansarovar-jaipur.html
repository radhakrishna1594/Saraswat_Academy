1. School overview

India International School (IIS), Mansarovar, Jaipur is a senior-secondary, co-educational school. The school says its foundation was laid on 16 July 1990. Its official website describes an academic environment that combines Indian cultural values with an international outlook and lists a range of academic, creative, leadership and sporting opportunities.

2. CBSE or international curriculum? Understand the difference

The IIS website presents India International School and IIS World School together. Parents should be clear about which pathway is suitable for their child, because curriculum, assessment, subject selection, admission process and costs can differ.

India International School (CBSE)

The CBSE school is listed as a senior-secondary institution. Families considering the Indian national curriculum should confirm the available subject combinations, class-wise admission vacancies, assessment pattern and senior-secondary subject choices directly with the school.

IIS World School

The school website also describes international pathways, including the Cambridge Pathway leading through IGCSE and the International Baccalaureate Diploma Programme (IBDP). Ask the school which programme and year group is available for the intended intake and confirm the relevant authorisation and programme details with the school and awarding organisation.

Before choosing a pathway, compare: the child’s future study plans, teaching and assessment style, subject flexibility, language requirements, transfer options, examination costs and the full fee schedule. Do not select an international curriculum only because it sounds more global.

3. Admissions for 2026–27

The school’s official website currently displays an admissions notice for the 2026–27 session. A circular dated 26 November 2025 announced admissions for selected entry points: Nursery/KG levels and Class 1 and Class 11 for India International School; and specified entry points for IIS World School, including CPP-1, IGCSE Year I and IBDP Year I. It also says requests may be made for vacancies in other classes.

Important: those published entry points are not a guarantee that seats remain available today. Contact the admissions office for the current vacancy, application window and exact class/board. The notice asks families to enquire with the school for other classes rather than assuming open admission.

Suggested admission steps

  1. Choose the exact school and curriculum: IIS (CBSE) or IIS World School (international pathway).
  2. Check the current class-wise admission notice and ask whether a seat is available.
  3. Confirm the age criteria, application form, any interaction or assessment, and the last date.
  4. Ask for the current fee schedule, transport routes and refund rules in writing.
  5. Submit the requested documents and keep copies of all forms, receipts and written communication.

Documents parents may need

  • Child’s birth certificate and recent photographs.
  • Previous school report card, where applicable.
  • Transfer certificate and other school records, if required for the class.
  • Parent/guardian identification and address proof as requested.
  • Any additional documents specified by the admissions office for the chosen curriculum.

For the latest notice, visit the official admissions page or contact the school directly.

5. Campus facilities and learning spaces

The official infrastructure page describes a multi-storey oval-shaped building, ventilated classrooms, science, mathematics and language laboratories, libraries, an auditorium, amphitheatre stage, recording studio, audio-visual room, resource centre, sports field and indoor activity areas. It also mentions solar and water-harvesting technologies.

The school lists dedicated areas for the library, STEM learning, ICT, science laboratories, Atal Tinkering Lab and Innovation Hub, medical room, sports, art, language learning and smart-board classrooms. Use the official pages to understand the facilities, then visit to see which are currently available to your child’s class and how often students use them.

  • Academic facilities: Ask how often students use the laboratories, library and ICT spaces in a typical timetable.
  • STEM and innovation: Ask whether students build, test and present projects themselves or mainly observe demonstrations.
  • Arts and performance: Ask about access to music, art, theatre, auditorium and recording facilities.
  • Sports and fitness: Check the activities offered to your child’s age group, coaching arrangements and regular practice time.
  • Health and safety: Ask about first aid, medical support, emergency response and parent notification.
  • Environment: If sustainability matters to your family, ask how the campus uses its solar and rainwater-harvesting systems.

6. Academics, results and student development

School choice should be based on the day-to-day learning experience as well as published achievements. Ask for the most recent board results for the exact programme and campus, including the number of students who appeared, pass percentage, subject-wise performance and the range of outcomes. Topper announcements alone do not show how an entire cohort performed.

During your visit, discuss:

  • How the school identifies and supports students who need additional help.
  • How frequently teachers assess progress and communicate it to parents.
  • How homework is balanced with reading, play, rest and extracurricular activities.
  • What clubs, leadership opportunities, debates, Model United Nations or community projects are available.
  • How students receive counselling and support during academic transitions.

The school’s website includes sections for progress and results, co-curricular activities, honours and achievements, and circulars. Use these official sections for current information rather than relying solely on social-media posts.

7. Transport, commute and hostel questions

The school website describes bus services and notes that the campus is near a metro station. Transport availability, route timings and fees are still route-specific and should be confirmed directly. A convenient school on a map may involve a long journey during Jaipur traffic, so test the commute at normal school travel times.

  • Does the bus cover your exact colony or pickup point this session?
  • What are the expected pickup and drop-off times, and how are delays communicated?
  • What adult supervision is provided while younger children board and leave the bus?
  • Is GPS tracking or another parent notification service available?
  • What is the process if an authorised adult is not available at drop-off?

The school’s admission circular has mentioned an off-campus hostel facility for girls from Grade 9 upwards. Because this is not the same as an on-campus hostel, parents considering it should request the current written details of the hostel’s location, operator, supervision, transport, safety procedures, meals, costs and eligibility before making plans.

8. Safety and student wellbeing

Safety is best assessed through current documents and observable procedures. The school’s mandatory public disclosure lists safety-related documents such as building safety, fire safety, water and sanitation information. Parents can request the latest valid documents and ask how the school handles daily safeguarding.

  • How are visitors identified and authorised to enter?
  • What are the student handover rules at the gate and on school buses?
  • How are bullying, harassment or repeated peer conflicts reported and handled?
  • Who is the designated contact for a child-safety concern or grievance?
  • What first-aid and emergency response arrangements are available during school hours?
  • How are parents informed about incidents, health concerns or changes to transport?

Also ask about counsellor access, support for students with different learning needs, and the process for arranging a confidential conversation with the relevant staff member.

9. Parent checklist before paying

  1. Am I applying to India International School (CBSE) or IIS World School (Cambridge/IB pathway)?
  2. Which class and curriculum are accepting applications now, and is a seat actually available?
  3. What is the current fee circular, including examination and transport charges?
  4. What are the class size, teacher allocation and student-support arrangements?
  5. How often do students use the labs, library, STEM spaces and sports facilities?
  6. What are the commute time, pickup arrangements and bus supervision?
  7. Can I see the latest affiliation and safety documents for the correct school?
  8. What is the withdrawal, fee-adjustment and refund policy?
  9. How are academic progress, attendance and parent concerns communicated?
  10. Can the school provide answers to my important questions in writing?

11. Frequently asked questions

What is the CBSE affiliation number of IIS Mansarovar?

The official CBSE SARAS record lists India International School, Mansarovar, Jaipur under affiliation number 1730055. It shows senior-secondary status and an affiliation period from 1 April 2025 to 31 March 2030. Check the official record for the latest details.

Is India International School the same as IIS World School?

The school website presents both under the Mansarovar campus, but they are associated with different curriculum pathways. Confirm the exact programme, awarding body, class and fee structure before applying.

Are admissions open for 2026–27?

The official website has displayed an admissions notice for 2026–27 with selected entry classes. Availability can change; contact the school to check current vacancies for the specific class and curriculum.

What are the current school fees?

Fees depend on the curriculum, class and session. Request the latest official fee circular, including transport, books, uniform, annual charges and any international examination fees. Do not rely on old third-party estimates.

Does the school have laboratories and sports facilities?

The official infrastructure page lists science, mathematics and language laboratories, libraries, STEM and ICT spaces, a sports field, auditorium and other learning facilities. Ask to see the spaces and confirm which are available to the class you are considering.

Where can I see school photographs?

Use the official infrastructure page and campus tour for school-provided visuals. The drawings on this page are original illustrations, not photographs of the actual campus.
