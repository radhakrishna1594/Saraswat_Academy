About Jayshree Periwal High School

Jayshree Periwal High School (JPHS) is a CBSE-affiliated co-educational school in Chitrakoot Scheme on Ajmer Road, Jaipur. The school provides education from the early years through senior secondary level and places emphasis on academics as well as activities beyond the classroom.

For a parent, the school is particularly relevant if you are looking for a school that combines a CBSE academic pathway with a broad programme of sports, arts, music, clubs, competitions and career-oriented preparation. The school also offers flexible boarding options for students who need them.

The school's current mandatory disclosure lists affiliation number 1730189, school code 10540, the Chitrakoot address and principal Madhu Maini.

Academic Programmes

The school follows a progression from pre-primary and primary education to middle school, secondary school and senior secondary education. Its academic information describes an approach that combines core subjects with art, music, dance, physical education, digital learning and other activities.

Classes 1–5

The primary programme focuses on foundational knowledge, communication, problem-solving, creativity and activity-based learning.

Classes 6–8

The middle-school programme develops subject understanding along with communication, scientific temper, arts, sports, social awareness and independent thinking.

Classes 9–10

Students prepare for the CBSE secondary-school stage while continuing to participate in activities that build communication, teamwork, leadership and time-management skills.

Classes 11–12

Students can pursue Science, Commerce or Humanities, with a range of optional subjects and combinations described by the school.

Admission Process

Admission requirements can change by class and academic session, so parents should check the school's current admission instructions before submitting an application. The school's admission information says that the process can include previous school records, assessment of English and Mathematics and an interview.

  1. Check the availability of seats for the required class.
  2. Complete the school's admission/application form.
  3. Keep previous school records and other required documents ready.
  4. For applicable classes, the student may have to take an English and Mathematics assessment.
  5. An interaction/interview may follow the assessment.
  6. If admission is offered, complete the fee and other formalities within the stipulated time.
Important: Admission criteria, age requirements, documents and seat availability should be confirmed directly with the school for the session in which you are applying.

Boarding Options

JPHS officially describes day, weekly and term boarding options. Weekly boarding is designed for students who stay during weekdays and return home for weekends, while term boarding provides a more residential arrangement with supervised study and activities.

  • Day school: Students attend the regular school programme and return home.
  • Weekly boarding: Students stay during the school week and go home for the weekend.
  • Term boarding: A longer residential option with supervised study and activities.
Boarding fees and availability are separate from the school fee table above. Ask the school for the current boarding fee and the class-wise availability before applying.

What Parents Should Check Before Admission

  • Confirm the exact class and number of seats available for the new session.
  • Ask for the complete first-year cost, including admission, transport, books, uniform and any optional services.
  • If considering boarding, ask specifically for the current boarding fee, accommodation arrangement and supervision details.
  • Check the subject combination carefully if the student is entering Class 11.
  • Visit the campus rather than relying only on photographs.
  • Ask how transport routes, timings and pickup points work for your location.
  • Read the school's current admission instructions and mandatory disclosures before submitting documents.
