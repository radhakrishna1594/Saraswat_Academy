About Modern School Jaipur

Modern School Jaipur is a CBSE-affiliated, co-educational English-medium senior secondary school at 79/1, Shipra Path, Sector 7, Mansarovar, Jaipur. CBSE records affiliation number 1730852, school code 11201, foundation year 2017 and the first opening date as 3 April 2017. The current school disclosure lists Ms. Kavita Bainsla as principal.

The Jaipur campus is part of the wider Modern School group whose history traces back to 1973. The group's published material lists campuses in Kota, Jaipur, Baran and Bharatpur, as well as The Steps play school in Kota. These are separate campuses/locations, so parents should check the affiliation, fees and admission rules for the particular campus they are considering.

Admission 2026–27

The Jaipur school's official admission page currently states that admissions are open for the 2026–27 session for Nursery to Class 9 and Class 11. For Nursery, the stated minimum age is 2.5 years. The school also describes separate audio-video rooms, a reading corner, activity corner and play area in its pre-primary wing.

The school says its teacher-student ratio is 1:20 on the admission page.

What parents should keep ready

  • Child's birth/age proof and the documents requested in the current admission form.
  • Previous school records for students seeking admission to higher classes.
  • Recent photographs and identity/address documents where required by the school.
  • Any transfer or board-related documents applicable to the class.
Important: Admission availability and document requirements can change. The current school admission page should be checked before submitting an application.

Academics & Subjects

The school follows the CBSE curriculum and describes technology-supported teaching aimed at developing critical thinking, creativity, intellectual curiosity and character building. Its published academic information lists English, Hindi, Science, Mathematics, Social Science, Computer Science, Physical Education and Sanskrit among the curriculum subjects.

For senior secondary classes, the school's affiliation documentation lists subjects including English, Hindi and French as language options and History, Political Science, Geography, Economics, Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Physical Education, Business Studies, Accountancy, Informatics Practices and Computer Science among the permitted subjects. The exact combinations available in a particular session should be confirmed with the school.

📚 Primary & Middle Years

Students build subject fundamentals while using activity-based and technology-supported learning opportunities. Parents should look at the child's individual learning needs rather than choosing a school only from its fee or infrastructure.

🎓 Secondary & Senior Secondary

Students move into the CBSE board pathway, with senior-secondary subject choices that can support different higher-education directions. Subject availability and combinations should be checked for the specific session.

School Facilities

The school describes interactive smart classes, a well-stocked library, cafeteria, centrally air-conditioned infrastructure and a 350-seat air-conditioned auditorium. Its mandatory disclosure currently lists 84 classrooms and nine laboratories, with internet facility available on campus.

💻 Smart Classrooms

Every classroom is described by the school as having an interactive smart-class setup with digital learning resources.

📖 Library

The school describes a well-stocked library with encyclopedias, classics, journals, reference books and periodicals.

🎭 Auditorium

The air-conditioned auditorium has a stated seating capacity of 350 and is used for theatre, debates, quizzes, music, dance and other student activities.

🍽️ Cafeteria

The school says specially designed meals are made available to support students' nutritional needs.

Fees at Other Campuses: What Is Verified

Because fees differ by campus, this guide does not copy Jaipur fees onto the other locations. For example, the official 2026–27 fee document for Modern School Kota lists admission fee of ₹20,000 and annual tuition totals ranging from ₹1,01,000 to ₹1,15,600 depending on the class group.

The latest fee document found for Modern School Naya Nohra on the official site is for 2025–26; it lists first-year totals for new students from ₹93,560 to ₹1,22,560 depending on the class group. It would be inappropriate to present those figures as 2026–27 fees until a newer official document is published.

For Bharatpur, the official fee document currently indexed is for 2024–25, so those older figures are not presented here as current fees.

Why this matters: School fees change. For a child's admission decision, use the current fee document issued by the specific campus rather than relying on old fee tables copied by third-party websites.

Modern School Jaipur: Questions Parents Should Ask

  • Is admission available for the required class in the current session?
  • What is the complete first-year cost after adding transport, books, uniform and other applicable charges?
  • Which exact subject combinations are available in Class XI?
  • What are the school timings and transport routes for our location?
  • What support is available for students who need additional academic or counselling support?
  • What activities and sports are compulsory and which are optional?
  • Can the family visit the campus and see the classrooms, labs, library and sports facilities?
