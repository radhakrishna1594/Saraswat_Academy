What is NEET UG?

National Eligibility-cum-Entrance Test (NEET-UG) is the common national entrance examination conducted by the National Testing Agency for undergraduate medical education. The official 2026 bulletin states that NEET is used for admissions to MBBS and BDS and also applies to BAMS, BUMS, BSMS and BHMS under the respective regulatory frameworks.

The examination has also become an important route for other programmes and institutions that use NEET results under their applicable rules. The exact admission eligibility always depends on the course, institution, counselling authority and current regulations.

Important: Dates, eligibility, fees, syllabus and admission rules can change. The current NTA information bulletin and the applicable regulatory/counselling authority are the final sources.

NEET UG eligibility

Age requirement

For NEET UG 2026, a candidate must have completed 17 years of age on or before 31 December of the year in which they appear. The 2026 bulletin states that there is no upper age limit.

Qualifying examination

Students who are appearing in the qualifying examination may apply and appear, but they must satisfy the qualifying requirements by the applicable counselling stage for admission. The prescribed subjects include Physics, Chemistry, Biology/Biotechnology and English, subject to the detailed qualifying-examination rules.

Class 12 students

Students appearing in Class 12 can apply under the applicable qualifying-examination code. Admission remains subject to passing the required qualifying examination and other conditions.

Indian citizens

Indian Nationals are eligible to appear subject to the applicable rules and qualifying conditions.

NRI / OCI / foreign nationals

The 2026 bulletin permits these categories to appear, with admission subject to the rules of the concerned government, institution and counselling authority.

NEET UG syllabus

The NEET syllabus covers Physics, Chemistry and Biology. The National Medical Commission notified the NEET UG 2026 syllabus, and the detailed syllabus is provided through the official NTA/NMC resources.

01

Physics

Build strong fundamentals and practise numerical applications. Focus on concepts, units, graphs, formula use and timed problem solving.

02

Chemistry

Balance physical chemistry numericals with inorganic concepts and organic reactions. Regular revision is essential because the subject mixes calculation and memory.

03

Biology

Study the prescribed Biology syllabus carefully, revise terminology and diagrams, and practise objective questions with close attention to textbook wording.

Best approach: Download the current official syllabus before making a long-term study plan. Do not assume that an old coaching list is identical to the current syllabus.

How to prepare for NEET UG

Phase 1 — Build conceptsComplete the prescribed syllabus systematically. Understand the theory before moving to large question banks.
Phase 2 — Strengthen practiceSolve chapter-wise MCQs and maintain an error notebook for concepts, calculation mistakes and avoidable errors.
Phase 3 — Add timed testsTake full-length tests regularly. Practise managing three subjects and protecting time for questions you can solve confidently.
Phase 4 — Analyse every testClassify mistakes as conceptual, factual, calculation, reading or time-management errors and revise accordingly.

A practical study cycle

NEET UG application process

Application is completed online through the official NTA/NEET portal. Students should read the current information bulletin before beginning the form and keep academic, identity and category documents ready.

Step 1 — Registration

Create the application record and carefully preserve the system-generated application number and login details.

Step 2 — Application

Enter personal and academic details, select the examination medium and cities as permitted, and upload the required documents.

Step 3 — Fee & confirmation

Complete the prescribed fee payment and make sure the Confirmation Page is generated. Keep a copy for future reference.

2026 reference: The official bulletin listed the application window as 8 February to 8 March 2026. Future-year dates must be checked from the current NTA notice rather than copied from this page.

What happens after NEET?

Qualifying NEET is followed by counselling and seat allocation. NTA conducts the examination and provides the result/rank; counselling is handled by the competent authorities according to the type of seat and course.

MCC / DGHS counselling

MCC handles counselling for the seats and institutions covered by its national counselling framework, including specified All India Quota and central/deemed-university pathways.

State counselling

State and Union Territory authorities conduct counselling for seats under their control according to their applicable eligibility, reservation and domicile rules.

AYUSH counselling

AACCC is the counselling authority for applicable All India Quota admissions to BAMS, BUMS, BSMS and BHMS under the relevant framework.

Do not confuse qualifying NEET with getting a particular college or branch. Admission depends on rank, category, seat availability, counselling choices, applicable reservation and the rules of the concerned authority.

Courses and career pathways through NEET

NEET is primarily associated with medical admission, but its result is used across several undergraduate healthcare pathways under the applicable regulations.

  • MBBS — Bachelor of Medicine and Bachelor of Surgery
  • BDS — Bachelor of Dental Surgery
  • BAMS — Bachelor of Ayurvedic Medicine and Surgery
  • BHMS — Bachelor of Homoeopathic Medicine and Surgery
  • BUMS — Bachelor of Unani Medicine and Surgery
  • BSMS — Bachelor of Siddha Medicine and Surgery
  • B.Sc. Nursing pathways where NEET is applicable under the relevant rules
  • Other programmes or institutions that are permitted to use NEET results under their published criteria

Before choosing a course, compare the curriculum, duration, regulatory framework, internship requirements, fees, location and the actual work involved in the profession.
