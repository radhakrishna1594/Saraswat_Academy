What is NDA & NA?

The National Defence Academy and Naval Academy Examination is conducted by the Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) for admission to officer-training pathways of the Indian Army, Navy and Air Force through the National Defence Academy, and to the 10+2 Cadet Entry Scheme of the Indian Naval Academy.

Eligibility for NDA & NA

Eligibility is notification-specific. The latest published detailed UPSC notice used for this guide requires candidates to satisfy nationality, age, marital-status, educational and service-specific conditions.

Nationality

Indian citizens and certain other categories specified by UPSC may be eligible, subject to the conditions in the notification.

Age

UPSC fixes an exact date-of-birth window for each examination. Do not calculate eligibility from a generic age range; use the DOB window printed in the current notice.

Marital status

The detailed NDA notice specifies unmarried male/female candidates for the eligible age group.

Education

For Army Wing, 12th class pass or appearing is required. For Air Force, Navy and Naval Academy, 12th with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics is required, subject to the current notification.

Important: The exact DOB range, vacancies and other conditions change by examination. Check the current UPSC notification rather than relying on an old article.

NDA Syllabus

Mathematics

Algebra, matrices and determinants, trigonometry, analytical geometry, differential and integral calculus, vector algebra, statistics and probability, and related senior-secondary mathematical concepts.

GAT: English

Grammar and usage, vocabulary, comprehension and the ability to understand and use English.

GAT: General Knowledge

Physics, chemistry, general science, social studies, geography, current events and other areas specified by UPSC.

The official syllabus is the controlling source. Build preparation around the latest UPSC syllabus and previous-year papers instead of relying only on coaching notes.

Army, Navy, Air Force & Naval Academy Pathways

Army Wing

Students meeting the Army eligibility conditions can pursue officer training through NDA and subsequently continue at the Indian Military Academy as applicable.

Naval Wing

Eligible candidates can enter the Naval pathway and continue professional training with the Indian Navy.

Air Force Wing

Eligible candidates can pursue Air Force training through the NDA pathway, subject to educational, medical and other requirements.

Naval Academy

The 10+2 Cadet Entry Scheme at the Indian Naval Academy is a separate pathway included in the NDA & NA examination.

How to Prepare for NDA

1. Build the Class 11–12 base

Strengthen Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, English and core general-knowledge concepts.

2. Study the UPSC syllabus

Break the syllabus into weekly targets and maintain a revision notebook for formulas, facts and mistakes.

3. Solve previous papers

Use official UPSC question papers to understand difficulty, recurring concepts and time pressure.

4. Practise timed tests

Regular full-length practice is essential because both written papers are time-bound objective tests.

5. Develop awareness

Follow major national and international events, defence developments, geography, science and history.

6. Prepare for SSB

Work on communication, confidence, teamwork, reasoning, leadership, fitness and genuine self-awareness rather than memorised answers.

How to Apply

  1. Read the latest NDA & NA notification published by UPSC.
  2. Register and complete the application through the current UPSC online application system.
  3. Enter personal, educational and other details exactly as required.
  4. Upload/submit the required information and documents in the prescribed format.
  5. Check the application carefully before final submission and keep the application details for future reference.
  6. Download the admit card when UPSC releases it.
Application portal: UPSC directs candidates to its new online application portal for examination applications.

Selection Process: Written Exam → SSB → Medical

1. Written Examination

Qualify Mathematics and GAT according to the standards fixed for the examination.

2. SSB Test / Interview

Shortlisted candidates undergo the Services Selection Board process, which assesses officer potential through multiple tests and interviews.

3. Medical Examination

Candidates must meet the prescribed medical and physical standards for the service they are seeking.

4. Final Merit & Allocation

Final selection and service allocation depend on merit, preferences, vacancies and applicable eligibility/medical conditions.
