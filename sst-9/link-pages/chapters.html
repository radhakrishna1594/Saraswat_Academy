Class 9 Social Science — Understanding Society: India and Beyond

Understanding Society: India and Beyond is the new NCERT Social Science textbook for Grade 9, introduced for the academic session 2026–27. Published by the National Council of Educational Research and Training (NCERT), this book presents Social Science as an interconnected study of society, people, places, institutions, resources, governance and economic life.

Unlike the earlier arrangement of separate textbooks for History, Geography, Political Science and Economics, this integrated textbook connects these disciplines to help students understand how different aspects of society influence one another. The book encourages students to observe their surroundings, ask questions, examine evidence, identify patterns and develop a deeper understanding of India and the wider world.

Key Features of the Book

  • Integrated approach to Geography, History, Political Science and Economics.
  • Designed according to the educational vision of NEP 2020 and NCF-SE 2023.
  • Focus on conceptual understanding rather than memorisation of isolated facts.
  • Encourages observation, questioning, reasoning and evidence-based learning.
  • Connects classroom concepts with everyday life and real-world situations.
  • Introduces students to India's geographical, historical, political and economic development.
  • Develops awareness of democracy, citizenship, markets, resources and social institutions.
  • Provides opportunities for discussion, exploration, interpretation and critical thinking.
  • Encourages students to understand society through multiple perspectives.

What Students Learn

The book helps students understand how the physical environment shapes human life, how civilisations and societies developed over time, how democratic institutions function, and how individuals and societies make economic choices. It also develops the ability to connect historical events, geographical processes, political institutions and economic decisions rather than studying them as completely separate subjects.

Skills Developed

  • Critical and analytical thinking
  • Historical and geographical reasoning
  • Understanding of democratic institutions and citizenship
  • Economic reasoning and decision-making
  • Interpretation of maps, evidence and information
  • Observation and inquiry-based learning
  • Problem-solving and application of concepts
  • Connecting classroom learning with real-life situations

Why This Book Is Important

Understanding Society: India and Beyond provides a broader and more connected way of learning Social Science at the secondary level. Instead of viewing History, Geography, Political Science and Economics as isolated subjects, students are encouraged to see the relationships between people, places, institutions, resources and events. This approach helps build informed, thoughtful and responsible learners who can understand society and participate meaningfully in the world around them.

Frequently Asked Questions — Class 9 Social Science

1. What is the Class 9 Social Science textbook called?

The Class 9 Social Science textbook is titled Understanding Society: India and Beyond.

2. Who publishes Understanding Society: India and Beyond?

The textbook is published by the National Council of Educational Research and Training (NCERT).

3. Which class is this Social Science book meant for?

This textbook is designed for Grade 9 (Class 9) students.

4. What is the full name of the book?

The full title is Understanding Society: India and Beyond — Social Science Textbook for Grade 9, Part 1.

5. Which subjects are covered in this textbook?

The book integrates major areas of History, Geography, Political Science and Economics into one Social Science textbook.

6. How many chapters are there in Part 1?

Part 1 contains 9 chapters covering important concepts related to society, geography, history, democracy and economics.

7. Is this a new NCERT Social Science textbook?

Yes. It is part of the newer NCERT textbook framework developed in line with the educational vision of NEP 2020 and NCF-SE 2023.

8. What is the main purpose of this book?

The book aims to help students understand society through connected ideas involving people, places, history, institutions, resources and economic activities.

9. Does the book focus only on memorising facts?

No. It encourages conceptual understanding, inquiry, observation, reasoning, discussion and application rather than simple memorisation.

10. What geographical concepts are introduced in the book?

The textbook introduces students to topics such as the Earth's surface, atmosphere and climate, helping them understand important physical processes and their relationship with human life.

11. What historical periods are covered in Part 1?

Part 1 introduces students to early humans, the beginning of civilisation, and the development of states and societies up to around 1000 CE.

12. Does the book teach students about democracy and elections?

Yes. The Political Science portion introduces important ideas related to democracy and elections and helps students understand democratic participation.

13. What economic concepts are covered?

The book introduces fundamental economic ideas such as choice, scarcity, prices and markets, helping students understand how economic decisions are made.

14. What skills does this textbook develop?

It develops critical thinking, analytical reasoning, interpretation, inquiry, problem-solving and the ability to connect classroom concepts with real-life situations.

15. Is Understanding Society: India and Beyond useful for Class 9 exam preparation?

Yes. It is the prescribed NCERT Social Science textbook and provides the foundational concepts students need for understanding the Class 9 Social Science curriculum and preparing for school examinations.
