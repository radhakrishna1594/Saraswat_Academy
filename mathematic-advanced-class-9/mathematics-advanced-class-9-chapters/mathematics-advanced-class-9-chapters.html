Mathematics at Advanced Level (Optional) – Grade 9

Welcome to the solutions section for Mathematics at Advanced Level (Optional), Grade 9 for the academic session 2026–27. This book is published by the Academic Unit, Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE).

This book introduces students to important mathematical ideas that go beyond routine calculations and encourages them to understand concepts, recognise patterns, develop logical thinking, and solve mathematical problems systematically.

About the Book

The book covers six important areas of mathematics. It begins with the concept of Sets and gradually moves towards topics such as Logarithms, Relations and Functions, Coordinate Geometry, Combinatorics and Progressions.

The chapters include definitions, explanations, examples, exercises and applications of mathematical concepts. Several topics are connected with situations from everyday life and other areas of study, helping students see how mathematical ideas can be applied beyond the classroom.

What You Will Learn

  • Sets: Understanding sets, their representation, subsets, power sets, cardinality, Venn diagrams and operations on sets.
  • Logarithms: Understanding logarithms and their properties, along with their applications in different areas.
  • Relations and Functions: Learning about ordered pairs, Cartesian products, relations, functions, graphs, domain and range.
  • Coordinate Geometry: Developing an understanding of mathematical concepts related to coordinates and the Cartesian plane.
  • Combinatorics: Learning systematic methods for counting arrangements and selections instead of listing every possibility.
  • Progressions: Exploring further ideas related to mathematical progressions and patterns.

How to Use This Solutions Website

The solutions provided on this website are designed to help students understand the questions step by step. Instead of simply looking at the final answer, students should first try the question themselves and then use the solution to check their method and understand the reasoning.

Each solution should be read carefully, especially the mathematical steps, definitions, formulas and reasoning used to reach the final answer. This approach can help students build a stronger conceptual understanding of the topics covered in the book.
