Active and Passive Voice: Rules and Examples

Voice shows whether the subject performs an action or receives it. Learn how to change active voice into passive voice, choose the correct verb form, and avoid common errors.

1. What Is Active Voice?

A sentence is in the active voice when its subject performs the action expressed by the verb.

Example: The gardener waters the plants.

  • Subject: the gardener
  • Verb: waters
  • Object: the plants

The sentence focuses on the person doing the action.

2. What Is Passive Voice?

A sentence is in the passive voice when its subject receives the action. The object of an active sentence often becomes the subject of the passive sentence.

Active: The gardener waters the plants.
Passive: The plants are watered by the gardener.

The meaning is similar, but the focus changes from the gardener to the plants. The doer can be omitted when it is unknown, obvious or unimportant: The plants are watered every morning.

3. Basic Rule for Changing Active into Passive

  1. Find the subject, verb and object in the active sentence.
  2. Move the object to the subject position.
  3. Use the correct form of be (am, is, are, was, were, been, being or be) with the past participle (V3) of the main verb.
  4. Use by + doer when the doer needs to be mentioned.
  5. Keep the tense and meaning of the original sentence.

Example: The chef prepared the meal. → The meal was prepared by the chef.

Active voicePassive voice
The students clean the room.The room is cleaned by the students.
Rita wrote a letter.A letter was written by Rita.
They will announce the result.The result will be announced by them.

4. Passive Voice in Different Tenses

In passive constructions, the main verb is in its past participle form (V3). The form of be changes according to the tense.

TenseActive examplePassive example
Simple presentShe makes cakes.Cakes are made by her.
Present continuousShe is making a cake.A cake is being made by her.
Present perfectShe has made a cake.A cake has been made by her.
Simple pastShe made a cake.A cake was made by her.
Past continuousShe was making a cake.A cake was being made by her.
Past perfectShe had made a cake.A cake had been made by her.
Simple futureShe will make a cake.A cake will be made by her.
Future perfectShe will have made a cake.A cake will have been made by her.
Modal verbShe can make a cake.A cake can be made by her.

Passive forms of some continuous and perfect continuous tenses are grammatically possible but are often awkward and rarely used in ordinary English. In school grammar, focus on the common forms above and follow the format required by your syllabus.

5. Changing Pronouns

Example: She helped me. → I was helped by her.

6. Negative Sentences

Keep the negative word not with the auxiliary verb in the passive sentence.

  • Active: They do not sell newspapers here.
    Passive: Newspapers are not sold here.
  • Active: He did not complete the task.
    Passive: The task was not completed by him.
  • Active: She will not accept the offer.
    Passive: The offer will not be accepted by her.

When changing a simple present or simple past sentence, the auxiliary used in the passive carries the tense, so do/does/did is not retained in the passive form.

7. Questions in Passive Voice

In questions, place the required auxiliary before the new subject. Keep the question mark.

  • Active: Does Rohan write the reports?
    Passive: Are the reports written by Rohan?
  • Active: Did the police catch the thief?
    Passive: Was the thief caught by the police?
  • Active: Will they invite you?
    Passive: Will you be invited by them?
  • Active: Who wrote this poem?
    Passive: By whom was this poem written? / Who was this poem written by?

8. Commands and Requests

Commands can be changed using Let + object + be + past participle in formal grammar exercises. A more natural passive construction may also be possible depending on the context.

  • Active: Open the door.
    Passive: Let the door be opened.
  • Active: Do not touch the wires.
    Passive: Let the wires not be touched.
  • Active: Please complete the form.
    Passive: You are requested to complete the form.

For polite requests, the passive form often changes the sentence structure rather than using a literal word-for-word conversion.

9. When Is Passive Voice Used?

  • When the doer is unknown: My bicycle was stolen.
  • When the action is more important: The road is being repaired.
  • In formal or scientific writing: The samples were examined in the laboratory.
  • When the doer is obvious: The suspect was arrested.

Use active voice when it makes a sentence clearer and more direct. Passive voice is useful when the receiver of the action deserves more attention.

10. Verbs That Usually Cannot Be Made Passive

A passive sentence normally needs a verb that takes an object. Verbs such as sleep, arrive, happen and die are usually intransitive in their common meanings and do not have an object to move into the subject position.

  • Correct: The baby slept peacefully.
  • Incorrect: Peacefully was slept by the baby.
  • Correct: An accident happened yesterday.
  • Incorrect: An accident was happened yesterday.

Some verbs can be used transitively or intransitively depending on their meaning, so decide from the way the verb is used in the particular sentence.

11. Common Mistakes

  • Using V2 instead of V3: Incorrect: The letter was wrote. Correct: The letter was written.
  • Forgetting the verb “be”: Incorrect: The room cleaned every day. Correct: The room is cleaned every day.
  • Changing the tense: Active: They are building a bridge. Correct passive: A bridge is being built by them.
  • Adding “by” unnecessarily: “The road was repaired yesterday” is often better than “The road was repaired by workers yesterday” when the doer is not important.
  • Trying to make every verb passive: Check whether the verb has an object that can become the passive subject.

14. Quick Revision

  • Active voice focuses on the doer of an action.
  • Passive voice focuses on the receiver of the action.
  • The object of an active sentence usually becomes the subject of the passive sentence.
  • Use the correct form of be + V3 in common passive structures.
  • Keep the tense, negative meaning and question form accurate.
  • Passive voice is normally possible only when the verb is used with an object.

Before converting a sentence, identify its tense and object. Then select the correct passive form and check that the meaning remains the same.
