Forms of Verbs: V1, V2, V3 and -ing

Verbs change their forms depending on how they are used in a sentence. Learning V1, V2, V3 and the -ing form helps you make correct sentences in different tenses and structures.

1. What are verb forms?

A verb expresses an action, event or state. Many English verbs have different forms. In school grammar, these are often shown as V1, V2, V3, and the -ing form.

FormNameExample: playCommon use
V1Base formplayPresent simple with I/you/we/they; after modal verbs
V2Simple pastplayedCompleted past actions
V3Past participleplayedPerfect tenses and passive structures
V-ing-ing form / present participleplayingContinuous tenses; also used as a gerund

For regular verbs, V2 and V3 often have the same form. Irregular verbs may change in different ways, so their forms need to be learned.

2. Regular verbs

Most regular verbs form V2 and V3 by adding -ed or -d to the base form.

V1V2V3V-ing
workworkedworkedworking

cleancleanedcleanedcleaning
livelivedlivedliving

stopstoppedstoppedstopping
carrycarriedcarriedcarrying

Spelling rules to remember

  • For many verbs, add -ed: talk → talked.
  • If the verb ends in a silent -e, add -d: hope → hoped.
  • For a consonant followed by -y, change y to i before -ed: study → studied. But before -ing, keep the y: studying.
  • In some short verbs ending consonant-vowel-consonant, double the final consonant: plan → planned, planning.

4. How to use each form

V1: Base form

  • I play cricket every Sunday.
  • They can solve this question.
  • Please open your books.

After modal verbs such as can, could, may, might, must, should and will, use the base form: She can sing, not She can sings.

V2: Simple past form

  • We visited Jaipur last week.
  • He wrote a letter yesterday.
  • They went home early.

Use V2 for a completed action in the past: She ate breakfast at seven.

V3: Past participle

V3 usually appears with a helping verb; it does not normally stand alone as the main verb in a standard perfect-tense sentence.

  • I have finished my homework.
  • She has written a poem.
  • The window was broken by the ball.

V-ing: The -ing form

  • He is reading a story.
  • We were waiting for the bus.
  • Swimming is good exercise.

The -ing form is used in continuous verb forms and can also function as a noun, as in Swimming is good exercise.

5. V2 and V3: understand the difference

V2 is used for the simple past. V3 is used with helping verbs in perfect tenses and passive constructions.

V2 exampleV3 example
She wrote the answer yesterday.She has written the answer.
They saw the match.The match was seen by thousands.
He ate lunch early.He has eaten lunch.

A useful test: if the sentence uses has, have or had for a perfect tense, the main verb generally takes V3.

6. Common mistakes

IncorrectCorrect
I have went to school.I have gone to school.
She did not went.She did not go.
He has wrote a letter.He has written a letter.
They are play football.They are playing football.
She can sings well.She can sing well.
We was happy.We were happy.

Remember: after did/did not, use V1 because did already shows past time.

7. Practice exercises

Try answering before opening the solutions.

  1. Write V2 and V3 of go.
    Show answer

    V2: went; V3: gone.

  2. Fill in the blank: She has ___ her work. (finish)
    Show answer

    finished — use V3 after “has”.

  3. Choose the correct form: We ___ the museum yesterday. (visit / visited / visiting)
    Show answer

    visited — “Yesterday” indicates the simple past.

  4. Correct the sentence: He did not ate breakfast.
    Show answer

    He did not eat breakfast.

  5. Write all four forms of write.
    Show answer

    V1: write; V2: wrote; V3: written; V-ing: writing.

  6. Fill in the blank: The children are ___ in the park. (run)
    Show answer

    running — continuous forms use the -ing form after “are”.

  7. Choose the correct form: I have ___ that film. (see / saw / seen)
    Show answer

    seen — use V3 after “have”.

  8. Correct the sentence: She can to dance.
    Show answer

    She can dance. Do not add “to” after the modal “can”.
