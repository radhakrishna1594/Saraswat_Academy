About Science

Science helps us understand the world around us. It explains why things happen, how nature works and how we use knowledge to solve everyday problems. From the food we eat and the air we breathe to electricity, machines and medicines, science is closely connected with our daily lives.

Studying science is not just about learning facts from a textbook. It is about asking questions, observing things carefully, performing experiments and understanding the reasons behind different phenomena. It encourages students to think logically and develop a curious and questioning mind.

Major Areas of Science

Branch What It Helps Us Understand Example
Physics Motion, forces, energy, light and electricity. How a fan rotates or a ball moves.
Chemistry Substances, their properties and how they change. Why iron rusts or water boils.
Biology Living organisms, their structure and life processes. How plants grow and how our body works.

Why Is Science Important for Students?

  • Develops curiosity, observation and logical thinking.
  • Helps explain natural events and everyday experiences.
  • Builds a foundation for higher studies in science and technology.
  • Encourages students to understand problems and find practical solutions.
  • Introduces important ideas used in healthcare, engineering and environmental protection.

Remember: Science becomes more interesting when you connect what you read in a book with what you see happening around you.

Importance of the NCERT Science Book

The NCERT Science textbook is an important resource for students studying the school science curriculum. It introduces scientific concepts in a structured way and helps students understand topics through explanations, illustrations, activities and questions.

Science is easier to understand when students do not simply memorise definitions. Reading the textbook carefully, studying diagrams and thinking about the examples can help make difficult concepts clearer. The activities in the book also encourage students to observe, investigate and learn through experience.

What Makes the NCERT Science Book Useful?

Textbook Feature How It Helps Students
Concept Explanations Helps students understand scientific ideas step by step.
Diagrams and Illustrations Makes structures, processes and scientific concepts easier to visualise.
Activities and Experiments Connects scientific theory with observation and practical learning.
In-text and Exercise Questions Provides opportunities to check understanding and practise applying concepts.

How to Study Science from NCERT

  1. Read the chapter carefully and understand the main ideas.
  2. Pay attention to diagrams, examples and scientific terms.
  3. Complete the activities safely, following your teacher's guidance.
  4. Answer the textbook questions in your own words.
  5. Revise important definitions, processes and diagrams regularly.

Study tip: After finishing a topic, close the book and explain it in your own words. If you can explain the concept clearly, you are more likely to have understood it.

A Practical Study Timetable for Science

Science includes concepts, diagrams, definitions, calculations and processes. Giving a little time to each of these regularly can make revision easier and help students avoid last-minute preparation.

The following is a suggested daily routine. Students can adjust the timings according to their school schedule, homework and other subjects.

Duration Study Activity Purpose
15 minutes Revise the previous lesson and important terms. Keep earlier concepts fresh.
30 minutes Read and understand a new topic from NCERT. Build conceptual understanding.
20 minutes Study diagrams, processes, activities or numerical examples. Strengthen visual and practical understanding.
25 minutes Answer textbook questions without looking at the solutions. Improve recall and answer-writing skills.
10 minutes Check mistakes and note topics for revision. Identify areas that need more attention.
Weekend: 45–60 minutes Revise completed topics and attempt a short practice test. Check progress and improve preparation.

Tips for Better Science Preparation

  • Understand the reason behind a process instead of memorising it blindly.
  • Practise drawing and labelling important diagrams neatly.
  • Learn scientific terms and definitions with their meanings.
  • Practise numerical questions by writing each step clearly.
  • Revise difficult topics more than once and ask your teacher when something is unclear.

Remember: A short, focused study session every day can be more useful than trying to revise an entire subject in one sitting.

Frequently Asked Questions About Science

1. How can I make Science easier to understand?

Start by understanding the basic concept and connect it with an everyday example. Read the explanation carefully, study the diagrams and try explaining the topic in your own words.

2. Is memorising answers enough to prepare for Science?

Memorising a few important terms can help, but understanding the concepts is equally important. Learn the reasons behind scientific processes so that you can explain them clearly and answer different types of questions.

3. How should I remember scientific definitions?

Understand the meaning first, identify the important scientific terms and revise them regularly. Try writing definitions from memory and then compare them with your textbook.

4. Are diagrams important in Science?

Yes. Diagrams can help explain structures and processes clearly. Practise drawing relevant diagrams with correct labels and understand what each part represents.

5. How should I prepare for Science numerical questions?

Identify the given quantities, write the required formula, substitute the values carefully and show the calculation steps. Practise similar questions to improve accuracy.

6. Should I read the NCERT textbook before using solutions?

Yes. Read the relevant topic and attempt the questions yourself first. Use solutions to understand difficult steps, check your answers and correct mistakes.

7. How much time should I study Science every day?

A focused session of around 1–2 hours can be a useful starting point for many students. Adjust the time according to your syllabus, learning needs and workload in other subjects.

8. What is the best way to revise Science before an exam?

Revise the main concepts, important terms, diagrams and formulas. Practise textbook questions and sample papers where applicable, then review the mistakes you make.

9. What should I do if I find a Science chapter difficult?

Divide the chapter into smaller topics. Start with the basics, use diagrams or examples to understand the ideas, and discuss confusing points with your teacher.
