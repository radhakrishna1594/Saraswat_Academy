Class 9 Mathematics — Ganita Manjari Part 2

Ganita Manjari – Part 2 is the second volume of the new NCERT Mathematics textbook for Grade 9. It continues Part 1 and contains Chapters 9 to 14, completing the 14-chapter Class 9 Mathematics textbook for the 2026–27 academic session.

Part 2 gives greater emphasis to mathematical reasoning, logical thinking, proof, data interpretation, algorithms, geometry, algebra and mensuration. Students are encouraged to understand relationships and justify results instead of relying only on memorised procedures.

Key Features of Ganita Manjari Part 2

  • Develops mathematical reasoning and logical thinking.
  • Introduces propositions, converses and mathematical justification.
  • Develops understanding of data through averages, combinations and graphical representation.
  • Introduces algorithms and step-by-step computational thinking.
  • Strengthens geometric reasoning through quadrilaterals and related theorems.
  • Develops understanding of linear equations in two variables and their graphical representation.
  • Extends mensuration to surface area and volume of three-dimensional shapes.
  • Encourages students to explain, investigate, compare and reason rather than only calculate.
  • Builds a foundation for Class 10 Mathematics and higher-level mathematical study.

Why Ganita Manjari Part 2 is Important for Class 9 Students

Ganita Manjari Part 2 moves students from routine calculation towards reasoning, representation and mathematical communication. The chapters ask students to examine statements, interpret data, understand algorithms, prove geometric relationships, represent linear relationships graphically and work with measurements of solid shapes.

Students should study the textbook carefully, work through its examples and activities, and attempt the exercises independently before referring to solutions. This approach helps build conceptual understanding and prepares students for competency-based questions.

A Practical Study Timetable for Ganita Manjari Part 2

Regular practice is one of the most useful habits for learning Mathematics. A planned study routine helps students revise concepts, solve questions and identify topics that need more attention.

The following timetable is a suggested daily routine. Students can adjust it according to their school timings, homework and individual learning needs.

Important Tips for Following the Timetable

  • Set a small, achievable target for every study session.
  • Keep a separate notebook for formulas and difficult questions.
  • Take short breaks when you feel tired.
  • Spend extra time revising concepts you find challenging.
  • Practise regularly instead of studying everything at the last moment.

Remember: The timetable is flexible. Consistent and focused practice is more important than following exact timings every day.

Frequently Asked Questions — Class 9 Mathematics Ganita Manjari Part 2

1. What is Ganita Manjari Part 2?

Ganita Manjari Part 2 is the second volume of the new NCERT Class 9 Mathematics textbook for the 2026–27 academic session. It contains Chapters 9 to 14.

2. How many chapters are there in Ganita Manjari Part 2?

Ganita Manjari Part 2 contains six chapters, numbered 9 to 14.

3. What is Chapter 9 of Ganita Manjari Part 2?

Chapter 9 is “Propositions and their Converses”. It develops mathematical reasoning through propositions, converses, examples, counterexamples and logical justification.

4. What is covered in Chapter 10?

Chapter 10, “How Quantities Combine: Understanding Data”, deals with ways of combining quantities and understanding data, including averages and weighted combinations and graphical representation.

5. What is Chapter 11 about?

Chapter 11, “The World of Algorithms”, introduces algorithms and systematic step-by-step procedures, including mathematical processes related to divisibility and the greatest common divisor.

6. What is Chapter 12 of Ganita Manjari Part 2?

Chapter 12 is “Quadrilaterals”. It develops geometric reasoning involving quadrilaterals, parallelograms, midpoint results, medians and the centroid.

7. What is Chapter 13 of Ganita Manjari Part 2?

Chapter 13 is “Two Variables, One Line”. It focuses on linear equations in two variables and their representation using graphs and algebraic methods.

8. What is Chapter 14 of Ganita Manjari Part 2?

Chapter 14 is “Math of Space: Surface Area and Volume”. It deals with surface area and volume of three-dimensional mathematical shapes.

9. Is Ganita Manjari Part 2 an NCERT textbook?

Yes. Ganita Manjari is the new NCERT Class 9 Mathematics textbook for the 2026–27 academic session, published in two parts.

10. Is Part 2 a continuation of Part 1?

Yes. Part 2 continues the chapter numbering from Part 1. Part 1 contains Chapters 1–8, while Part 2 contains Chapters 9–14.

11. Does Part 2 focus on mathematical reasoning?

Yes. Reasoning is especially important in topics such as propositions, converses, algorithms and geometry. Students are encouraged to justify conclusions rather than only state answers.

12. Does Ganita Manjari Part 2 include data?

Yes. Chapter 10 focuses on understanding data and how quantities combine, including averages and weighted combinations.

13. Does Part 2 include algorithms?

Yes. Chapter 11, “The World of Algorithms”, introduces algorithmic thinking and mathematical procedures.

14. Does Part 2 include geometry?

Yes. Chapter 12 is devoted to quadrilaterals and develops important geometric relationships and reasoning.

15. Does Part 2 include linear equations?

Yes. Chapter 13 studies linear equations involving two variables, including their graphical representation and solution methods.

16. Does Part 2 include surface area and volume?

Yes. Surface area and volume are covered in Chapter 14, “Math of Space: Surface Area and Volume”.

17. How should students study Ganita Manjari Part 2?

Students should read the concept first, work through examples and activities, attempt exercises independently, check their reasoning, and then revise questions that require more practice.

18. Is Ganita Manjari Part 2 useful for Class 9 examinations?

Yes. Students should use the prescribed textbook as a primary study resource and practise its concepts, examples and exercises thoroughly.

19. What academic session does Ganita Manjari Part 2 belong to?
