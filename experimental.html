About the Program

What is the IIT Madras School Connect Program?

The IIT Madras School Connect Program is an outreach initiative of the Centre for Outreach and Digital Education (CODE), IIT Madras. It aims to help school students explore academic subjects and career fields beyond their regular school curriculum.

Students can get introductory exposure to subjects such as data science, engineering, design, ecology, humanities and economics through structured online learning.

These courses can help students explore their interests and learn more about different areas of higher education.

Before You Apply

Eligibility and Important Information

Who can participate?

  • Students currently studying in Classes IX, X, XI or XII
  • Students from schools partnered with the program
  • Students who meet the specific course requirements
  • Students who follow the official enrollment procedure

What should students know?

  • Courses are generally designed for an 8-week learning period
  • Learning includes recorded and live online components
  • Assignments and assessments may be required
  • Certificates depend on meeting the applicable requirements
Important: Course availability, fees, eligibility, assessment rules and registration dates can change between batches. Confirm all current details directly with IIT Madras School Connect before enrolling.

Frequently Asked Questions

IIT Madras School Connect FAQs

Common questions from students and parents exploring the program.

1. What is IIT Madras School Connect?

It is an outreach initiative of the Centre for Outreach and Digital Education (CODE), IIT Madras, designed to introduce school students to different academic subjects and career fields.

2. Which classes are eligible?

The program is intended for students in Classes IX, X, XI and XII from partnered schools. Students should check individual course eligibility before registering.

3. Are the courses online?

The program uses online learning, including recorded lectures and live interactive sessions. Check the official program information for the details of a particular course and batch.

4. How long are the courses?

The standard course duration is 8 weeks. Students should confirm the schedule for their selected batch.

5. Can students register individually?

Students should contact their own school. The official program requires school partnership and provides enrollment instructions through the partnered institution.

6. Is there a course fee?

The official partnership information describes a nominal fee for students enrolling in certification courses. Check the official website for the current amount and payment instructions for your batch.

7. Will students receive a certificate?

The program provides certificate opportunities subject to successful completion of the applicable course requirements. Review the current assessment and certification rules before enrolling.

8. Where can I check the latest courses?

Visit the official IIT Madras School Connect website for the latest course list, eligibility details, registration and academic calendar.
