About Saraswat Academy

Saraswat Academy is a free educational platform dedicated to helping CBSE students from Class 6 to 12 through high-quality NCERT Solutions, Notes, MCQs, Worksheets, Practice Papers, Previous Year Questions and easy-to-understand study material. Our mission is to make quality education accessible to every student regardless of financial background.

🎯 Our Mission


Our mission is to simplify education and help students build strong conceptual understanding instead of rote learning. Every article published on Saraswat Academy is designed to be simple, reliable and useful for school examinations as well as competitive exams.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions


Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Saraswat Academy and its study resources.

1. What is Saraswat Academy?

Saraswat Academy is an educational platform that provides students with NCERT Solutions, notes, MCQs, worksheets, practice papers, previous year questions and other study resources to support learning and examination preparation.

2. Is Saraswat Academy free for students?

Yes. Most educational resources on Saraswat Academy are freely available for students. You can explore the available study materials and use them to support your daily studies and exam preparation.

3. Which classes are covered on Saraswat Academy?

Saraswat Academy provides educational resources for CBSE Classes 6 to 12. Study materials are being expanded regularly, and the availability of resources may vary by class and subject.

4. Which subjects are available on Saraswat Academy?

Saraswat Academy offers resources for various school subjects, including Mathematics, Science and other subjects. Students can explore the relevant class and subject sections to find available study materials.

5. What study materials are available on Saraswat Academy?

Students can explore NCERT Solutions, chapter-wise notes, MCQs, worksheets, practice papers, previous year questions (PYQs), important questions and other educational resources, depending on the class and subject.

6. Are NCERT Solutions available chapter-wise?

Yes. Saraswat Academy provides chapter-wise NCERT Solutions for available classes and subjects. Students can select their class, subject and chapter to find textbook exercise solutions and understand the concepts more effectively.

7. Can I use Saraswat Academy for exam preparation?

Yes. Students can use NCERT Solutions, notes, MCQs, worksheets and practice papers to support their examination preparation. For effective preparation, follow your prescribed syllabus, practise regularly and revise important concepts.

8. Does Saraswat Academy provide MCQs and worksheets?

Yes. MCQs and worksheets are among the educational resources offered on Saraswat Academy. They help students practise questions, revise concepts, test their understanding and identify topics that need more attention.

9. Does Saraswat Academy provide previous year questions and practice papers?

Yes. Previous year questions and practice papers are among the resources available on Saraswat Academy. Students can use them to practise examination-style questions, revise topics and become familiar with different question formats.

10. Can I use Saraswat Academy for self-study?

Yes. Students can explore available resources independently and study at their own pace. Reading explanations, practising questions and revising difficult topics can support self-study alongside regular classroom learning.

11. Can I access Saraswat Academy on my mobile phone?

Yes. You can visit Saraswat Academy using a web browser on your mobile phone, tablet, laptop or desktop computer and explore the educational resources available on the website.

12. How can I find study materials for my class and subject?

Visit Saraswat Academy and navigate to the relevant class and subject section. Select the resource you need, such as NCERT Solutions, notes, MCQs or worksheets, and open the relevant chapter or topic if it is available.
